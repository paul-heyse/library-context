# Plan a functional-extension review

Prepare an inquiry that can discover useful capabilities and assess which are worth pursuing.
Load [functional-extension-review](../functional-extension-review/SKILL.md) for its assessment
contract, standard and publication routes. This preparation is ordinarily conversational and
does not create another durable document or switch runtime modes.

## Preserve the operator's exploration scope

Identify the selected outcome, workflow, subsystem or library/tooling ecosystem and the supplied
priorities or hypotheses. A broad request to explore an ecosystem is sufficient; discovering
valuable applications is part of the review. Clarify material ambiguity without requiring the
operator to supply a feature list or choosing a different focus for them.

Frame functional value across product, developer, operator and research activities as relevant.
Keep current commitments distinguishable from proposed extensions. The diagnostic audit and
existing design gaps can inform a question, but neither is a prerequisite or the boundary of
the opportunity space.

## Shape inquiry around decisions

Establish enough current context to identify relevant capabilities, consumers and integration
surfaces. Consider where a useful capability may be absent, difficult to use or newly enabled
by a combination of mechanisms. Available overviews can reveal unfamiliar options; their table
of contents need not become the review's assignment structure.

Direct investigation toward questions that could change functional value or the preferred
realization. What could someone accomplish afterward? Which existing strengths should survive?
Where could native or composed capabilities avoid unnecessary machinery? Which prerequisite or
operational obligation could change the recommendation? Leave conclusions open to evidence.

Choose source inspection, workflow scenarios, library/tooling research or independent advice
according to the uncertainty. Probes and measurements are discretionary unless a concrete claim
needs them. No broad audit, numerical scoring or benchmark campaign follows from preparation.

Use the [shared roles and coordination contract](../../../.agents/roles/README.md) when bounded
inquiries benefit from context isolation, distinct capabilities or independent judgment. Briefs
carry the exploration scope, relevant current baseline, supplied hypotheses and useful sources.
Ask for concrete enabling mechanisms and source pointers alongside the functional argument;
preserve their role in the eventual recommendations. Keep one reviewer able to reconcile shared
premises, interactions and conflicting proposals.

## Present an adaptable approach

Explain investigation priorities, consequential questions, assumptions and scope in the
conversation. Use the available planning interface when helpful. Preparation is sufficient when
useful inquiry can begin and material uncertainty has a route; further structure can emerge from
the review. Follow the operator's boundary between preparation and conducting it.

A requested recommendation audit can reassess the completed review's value, scope, realization
and omissions using the existing net-change and integrated-review convention. Do not insert it
as an unrequested stage. Selected opportunities proceed through create-plan; review preparation
does not authorize implementation, installation or changes to accepted scope.
