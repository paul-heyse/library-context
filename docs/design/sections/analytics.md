# Analytics

**Implemented; Phase 4 qualification in progress, 2026-10-01.** The typed Structural and Analytic
owners are `lctx-model::domain::structural` and `domain::analytics`. Their declarations own
semantic inputs, output identity, qualification, coverage, parameters and stored replay.
`cpg-core::structural` and `analytic` acquire completed inputs and publish results;
`analysis_graphs` prepares the borrowed computational snapshots.
`lctx-analytics`' four native kernel entry points expose model-owned scheduling, ranking,
concept and neighbor operations; they do not create a second semantic authority.
The [Phase 4 plan](../../plans/semantic-model-phase4-detailed-plan_2026-09-30.md) owns acceptance.

> Decision: ADR-0085, ADR-0086, ADR-0106, ADR-0108

<a id="section-9"></a>

## §9 Analytics

Structural methods answer public access, delegation, direct controls, usage and handoff questions.
Optional topology/concept methods answer ranking, grouping and shared-attribute questions.
Neither class is a source parser. Mandatory C0/C1 catalog and evidence construction is independent
of brief seeds, optional analytics and embeddings. C2 selection and S0 preserve each source's
meaning; heuristics cannot establish a control, behavioral negative or execution fact.

Normalized relations own semantic graph endpoints and arcs. The projection snapshot owns the
bounded immutable petgraph object, canonical ordering and unresolved side records. Consumers
borrow its native visit views and retain ArcId lineage, isolates and parallel evidence.
Relational questions remain typed relations/DataFusion; no graph schema or path cache becomes
an independent authority. Each lifecycle rebuilds its computational snapshots from canonical
relations.

Every method records input/context, definition and parameters, projection/version, universe,
output selector, direction, multiplicity, weight policy, seed, library identity and diagnostics.
Structural outcomes are derived and replayed by `domain::structural::outcomes` over all four exact
method invocations and retained stop inventories (**Implemented, focused-Tested 2026-10-04**;
actual publication adversarial controls pending). Core publishes those results without interpreting
status. Source coverage is a separate fold. Conditional argument flows compose call and Local
qualifications, including canonical conditions and assumption sets; publication reads immutable Local
premises separately from current Structural vocabulary.

A selected method's unsupported/budget outcome is explicit. Disabled techniques publish
NotRequested; missing vectors or candidates are not an empty successful analysis. A nominal
Analytic frame links the complete selected technique set and its outcomes.

Defaults keep communities, PageRank, FCA/RCA, analytic kNN and extra community layers off. RCA
requires FCA; extra community layers require communities. Technique recovery does not establish
product benefit or activate performance/ablation campaigns.

**Implemented, 2026-10-02 (ADR-0116).** `domain::analytics::policy::RETAINED` is the one fixed
policy for parameters, complete definition identity and executable kernel settings. It retains
PageRank damping/tolerance/work, all four community resolutions and ten seeds with their quality
histories, concept support/bounds and neighbor floor/count. Method records do not pretend the
forty community runs are one run. The human recipe is generated from the structured policy.
`DependencyClosure` preserves exact ordered epoch requirements independently of sufficient stage
grants. Analytic consumption resolves those source receipts through separate sessions. Prepared
graphs remain borrowed and checked for each consumer. CPU placement and independent RSS sampling
are operational; they do not change analytic content or establish a speed benefit.

> Decision: ADR-0044, ADR-0045, ADR-0071, ADR-0086, ADR-0106, ADR-0116, ADR-0121

<a id="section-9-1"></a>

### §9.1 Public entry point and delegation

The Structural owner derives public candidates from normalized exposures and C0 members. Public
access identity belongs to the access slot, not the declaration that currently resolves it.
Known aliases, inherited origins and unresolved targets retain their attributed evidence.

Delegation traversal uses the stored invocation projection with the selected direction, hop and
work bounds. It retains exact target alternatives, source call sites, parallel arcs and explicit
frontier stops. A path's qualification is no stronger than its premises. Conservative topology
can schedule/traverse a target without admitting it as a behavioral callee. Direct usage is a
separate typed policy, not graph degree or delegation depth.

<a id="section-9-2"></a>

### §9.2 Controls and local restrictions

`structural::controls` owns control paths, traversals and ordered steps, argument flows, literal
arguments, conditional raises and unfollowed boundaries. Formal/entity links, binding sources,
Entry-value witnesses and original predicate/raise occurrences retain exact context. S0 renders
these records directly; generic labels cannot replace parameter, literal or guard premises.

An actual may reach a callee unchanged, as a derived value, or through an open boundary. Default
and receiver roles remain distinct. A conditional raise retains its exact guard and source raise
statement; it does not establish operation-wide rejection or escaping exceptions without the
behavioral completion proof. A deeper call, unsupported construct or exceeded budget is an
explicit unfollowed path/argument. Name proximity and signature resemblance establish nothing.

<a id="section-9-3"></a>

### §9.3 Direct handoff

A direct handoff connects admitted source producer/consumer places under its qualification and
cited events. Identity and Derived value relationships remain distinct from control influence.
Normalized receiver/binding evidence determines ownership; matching field names cannot manufacture
cross-method storage or reader identity.

Symbolic source field associations can enrich the catalog and Summary uncertainty inventory.
They do not prove allocation, intervening alias/mutation stability or later runtime values.
Direct handoff conclusions and unresolved associations retain separate sources and coverage.

<a id="section-9-4"></a>

### §9.4 Community detection

The model-owned community adapter converts selected snapshot layers to `leiden-rs::GraphDataBuilder`
with declared direction/symmetrization, parallel collapse, weighting, normalization and lineage.
ArcId weights are evidence identifiers and are not numeric algorithm weights. The admitted pair
universe retains the existing touched-vertex policy and discloses excluded isolates.

Resolution/seed alternatives, selected partition, score/convergence or unavailable diagnostics,
and layer membership are typed outputs. Compare partitions by membership, not algorithm numeric
labels. Optional type, mention and neighbor layers require their own admitted evidence and
parameters. Official document co-mentions use either the frame's own input or a corpus explicitly
linked to that library through `CorpusLibrary`; input names or shared generation do not establish
membership. Exact analysis context, resolved entity universe and one vote per passage/entity
remain required. This association correction is **Implemented**, 2026-10-03; actual split-input
qualification is pending in the code-facts coordinator §7.
A community label is a deterministic description; membership remains heuristic and
cannot assert behavior or capability identity.

> Decision: ADR-0020, ADR-0106

<a id="section-9-5"></a>

### §9.5 Centrality

The weighted PageRank kernel retains damping, dangling treatment, iteration/tolerance bounds and
residual diagnostics. Conversion follows the selected directed weighted projection; an unweighted
library routine is not an interchangeable definition. Results retain universe and output-selector
identity and remain heuristic.

Direct-usage ranking counts each distinct official-usage call site once and divides its share
among distinct admitted targets before subsystem/public filtering. It is not an arc count. Open
or incomplete target coverage is retained alongside a fractional score. The native ranking owner
provides the canonical operation to production and replay; display order does not strengthen
resolution certainty.

<a id="section-9-6"></a>

### §9.6 Formal and relational concept analysis

FCA builds a bounded typed object/attribute context using equal-length FixedBitSet domains and
the retained closure/NextClosure kernel. An attribute states its source, interpretation,
modality, evidence and applicability. Different evidence bases cannot merge because their labels
match. Extents/intents, implications and enumeration diagnostics are exact under that admitted
context, not universal runtime laws. The fcars development oracle compares concept sets.

**Implemented / scoped Tested, 2026-10-03:** model-owned source-parameter correspondence
supplies declared parameter/type incidence and bound-receiver exclusion. Native signature,
parameter and return attributes keep their invocation role, adjustment and native receiver;
`AttributePolicy` records role/receiver selection in the method recipe and digest. The retained
default is SourceDeclared with source bound receivers excluded and native slots preserved.
Native slots are already adjusted by their callable type and are not adjusted again.

Resolved decorators, class/member/record traits, deprecation, capture dependence and exit/terminal
typing have distinct attributed incidence sources. These remain characterization; capture timing
or terminal typing alone does not prove runtime value stability or completion. Boolean incidence
deduplicates membership while retaining its multiple supports. The sparse type layer still ranges
over release-defined classes, excluding builtin/dependency classes; richer observations do not
silently expand its universe. The [code-facts coordinator](../../plans/code-facts-expansion-plan_2026-10-03.md#7-current-checkpoint-and-next-action)
owns the focused native/real-PG P0/P1 receipts and pending assembled Q0 qualification.

RCA performs one declared relational scaling step over admitted calls/handoffs and the same FCA
context, followed by bounded concept enumeration. It requires FCA and preserves relation roles,
qualification and closure. No recursive ontology expansion is activated. Shared-signature or
applicable-case suggestions are exposed only through their typed conclusions and evidence policy.

> Decision: ADR-0020, ADR-0058, ADR-0106

<a id="section-9-7"></a>

### §9.7 Embeddings in analytics

Analytic text uses normalized declaration/document inputs, before synthesized briefs or retrieval
units. `embedding::analytic` owns its consumption records; retrieval has a separate later
consumption owner. Both refer to one immutable embedding spec and canonical exact value codec.
Spec/input hash, consumed text, availability and winning vector bytes are explicit.

The bounded exact neighbor kernel validates equal dimensions and finite admitted values before
dot products. Threshold, top-k and semantic-ID tie-breaking are declared. Mismatched widths refuse;
zip truncation is not similarity. Neighbor output can supply documentary links or a selected
community layer, never behavioral evidence. No dense all-pairs authority is persisted.

The realization adapter uses `lctx-embed` and the retained cache, with token admission before
service/cache effects and exact cache keys. Cold stored-value replay needs no live service.
Optional unavailability remains explicit and does not rewrite another completed invocation.
Live embedding qualification, performance and ranking benefit are not claimed here.

> Decision: ADR-0020, ADR-0106

<a id="section-9-8"></a>

### §9.8 Determinism and ablation

Canonical semantic IDs and ordering govern ties, SCCs, witness membership, weights, partitions,
concept enumeration and result bytes. Native graph indices and algorithm labels are private.
Named work/node/vertex/edge/witness limits distinguish semantic refusal from convergence and
transport failure. Parameters and executable sources enter the canonical definition digest.
Stored validators re-run the same kernels over completed input closure.

The four native entry points (`native_schedule`, `native_ranking`, `native_concepts`,
`native_neighbours`) delegate to the model-owned operations. Community conversion and the other
analytic methods are owned there too. Legacy pass/Arrow graph engines are not current producer
contracts. Their removal does not retire the retained capability, independent fixture or
uncertainty obligations.

Ablation, controlled pilot costs and product benefit comparisons remain separate future evidence.
Implementation and focused receipts do not establish measurement or differentiation; current
Phase 4 qualification remains in progress. [§14.12](api-and-evidence-product.md#section-14-12)
owns the product acceptance protocol.

> Decision: ADR-0020, ADR-0071, ADR-0106
