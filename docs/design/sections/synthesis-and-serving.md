## §10 Synthesis and briefs

S0 is the single writer of shared findings and programmatic assertions. Its independently known
native frame universe fixes the required C0, C1, C2, Structural, Analytic and Summary parents.
Each parent is a completed nominal invocation/frame under the exact selected input, context and
canonical definition. Optional NotRequested analyses remain distinct outcomes; a missing parent
is not fabricated as successful completion.

Model-owned pure operations prepare documentary conclusions, retain Summary facets, translate
qualified Structural/Analytic conclusions, emit findings/assertions, select configured and finite
automatic seeds, and render canonical briefs. `cpg-core::synthesis` acquires and publishes that
closure once. Replay reconstructs the same inputs and outputs; the source digest includes the
actual templates, original-code/setup operations and all deterministic emitters.

Mandatory catalog and retrieval construction covers public/evidence units independently of
brief selection. A seed without an admissible documentary Outcome produces `NoDocumentedOutcome`
omission while its catalog member and evidence remain available. Unrequested techniques or
missing optional embeddings do not erase the public universe.

**Implemented reuse, 2026-10-02.** S0 frame admission and automatic selection borrow one
`AnalyticParents` inventory containing exactly analytic frames, invocations and technique results.
Each operation retains its own declared inputs; seed replay constructs its independent parent
inventory. Actual consumed relations are completed immutable inputs; sharing does not authorize an
unfinished or different source universe. ADR-0128 replaces the earlier persisted epoch mechanism.

<a id="section-10-1"></a>

### §10.1 Findings

A finding is a typed conclusion, never a sentence. `analysis::findings` owns its kind, nominal S0
invocation, exact subject, qualification, coverage and member/support digests. Members name their
roles; supports name Support or Scope and a nominal source. Typed derivations retain ordered
premise membership and source witnesses. Generated derivation views expose explanations without
becoming another writable authority.

Earlier owners publish qualified conclusions. S0 alone turns those into shared findings using the
common evidence floor/ceiling and qualification operation. Structural observations retain exact
source premises. Analytic grouping/ranking remains heuristic. A Summary finite value or complete
call question retains its earlier nominal derivation, channel, phase, scope and verdict. A checked
RefutedUnderModel proof becomes `BehavioralRefutation`; Unknown or proof=None remains a facet
without finding authority.

<a id="section-10-2"></a>

### §10.2 Assertions

`ProgrammaticAssertion` identifies its S0 invocation, public member frame, typed template/version,
kind, section, qualification, evidence status and deterministic text. Its support cites the exact
documentary, Structural, Analytic, Summary or authored-code source. Text is a rendering of those
records, never a source from which semantics are reconstructed.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| StructurallyObserved | Supported by admitted extracted/derived structural evidence |
| Documented | Authored official docstring, document or example applicability |
| StatisticallyDerived | A qualified heuristic result with its selected method/context |
| FixtureChecked | An explicit executed observation for its stated inputs/environment |
| Unresolved | The required evidence does not establish the assertion |

`analysis::policy` owns the kind→section/status policy and shared derivation rule. Producers,
publication and replay use it directly. Heuristic support may describe grouping, ordering,
Related entries or documentary similarity; it cannot assert a control, behavioral limit or
negative. Fixture status requires an actual task observation, not extraction success.
`BehavioralRefutation` belongs in Limits at the StructurallyObserved floor, preserving the
original checked Summary qualification and exact proof/support chain.

Control text retains exact A0 path/traversal/step, argument flow, literal binding, entry-value,
conditional-raise and unfollowed premises. Parameter names follow qualified signature/entity
links; literal and guard/raise text uses original source occurrences. Source and effective
signature options are distinct. An unproved correspondence cannot be supplied by their labels.

**Implemented premise rendering, 2026-10-03 (ADR-0120).** Summary assertion and brief text
consume the actual shared qualification. A nonempty basis is explicitly conditional and identifies
its premise set; an empty basis states that no additional typing/closed-world assumptions apply.
It does not remove the finite model, source-body entry, condition or scope. Conservative and
restricted alternatives remain separate, and fresh runtime source-binding admission refuses a
nonempty typing basis. Current packet/runtime acceptance is owned by the code-facts coordinator.

<a id="section-10-3"></a>

### §10.3 Brief structure and the Outcome order

One canonical brief is anchored to one selected public member and its admissible Outcome.
Sections retain their atomic assertion membership:

| Section | Sources |
|---|---|
| Outcome | Admitted authored docstring or exact C1-associated passage lead |
| Public access | Public exposures, aliases and qualified delegation conclusions |
| Applicable case | Admitted typed applicability/positive Summary or concept conclusion |
| Controls | Declared parameter/options and exact Structural control premises |
| Usage pattern | Authored source pattern, official scenario or admitted handoff |
| Limits | Boundaries, documented warnings and proof-backed behavioral refutations |
| Evidence | Exact support/source and qualification references |
| Related | Qualified optional community/concept/neighbor navigation |

Outcome order is the entry docstring's first sentence, then the lead sentence of an exact
member-associated documentary passage whose mention lies within that sentence. Otherwise the
brief is omitted explicitly. Whitespace-collapsed sentence display retains original byte ranges;
changelogs and nearest-by-vector passages never supply an Outcome. Documentation-only briefs
are explicitly marked, rather than pretending to have behavioral support.

Native Warning and ParamField templates require the exact C1 public-member association and
native component/attribute premises. ParamField uses the original lead span; Warning retains the
original inner span, literal title where available, and nearest ParamField label when applicable.
Option lookup keeps source/effective signature namespaces distinct. Unknown or ambiguous fields,
nested parameter fields, inline components, unavailable native evidence and foreign context retain
explicit component boundaries. Fenced/inline code cannot become component authority; forged or
out-of-container spans refuse. These are documentary applicability claims, not runtime controls
or negative proofs. A library using another document convention has no inferred component facts.

Configured and automatic seed decisions retain their own public slot, documentary summary,
official-usage/rank/community basis and selected/refused cause. Navigation scores cannot become
semantic support. The canonical document retains ordered assertion, documentary, Summary-facet
and code-boundary links and a content digest. Bounded UTF-8 parts are stored whole; an indivisible
part exceeding its declared bound refuses rather than silently truncating. Embedding token
admission is a separate later operation. Briefs are canonical unreviewed renderings.

<a id="section-10-4"></a>

### §10.4 Grounding checks

Shared model validators reconstruct the documentary, Summary, assertion, finding and brief
closure from completed nominal inputs. A coupled deletion of a source and its displayed output
cannot evade replay. Kind/status, member/context/scope, proof qualification, original bytes,
source membership, ordered parts and digests remain independently checked. Missing required
relations or corrupt bytes are refusal, not an empty valid brief.

Canonical source artifacts and chunks own original bytes. Prose slices authenticate their range,
content and source qualification; C1 association qualification remains distinct from native
artifact evidence. A later rendering never extends a completed provider vocabulary or invents
a source span. The unresolved/omitted inventory identifies evidence gaps without treating them
as negative behavior. Phase 5 structured and Markdown representations must hydrate the same
support closure.

<a id="section-10-5"></a>

### §10.5 Usage patterns

Authored patterns preserve original source occurrences and required setup. `source_code` admits
simple unindented materialized Python fences only after verifying the complete original and
materialized bytes. Original Markdown/MDX and extracted Python coordinates stay distinct;
every translated statement must be an actual occurrence of the admitted artifact. An
unmaterialized MDX/non-Python fence retains MissingMaterialization; transformed, unsupported or
foreign mappings retain their specific boundary.

`source_setup` closes the smallest admitted statement/dependency subset from exact import,
binding, execution and source premises. Setup, prior mutation, exceptions and context structure
cannot be removed to make an example appear runnable. Supported native call/property/constructor
patterns retain actual roles, arguments, defaults, source surface and constructor/reader
association. Structural extraction does not execute the snippet or prove temporal heap identity.
Unsupported patterns remain explicit. TaskReport observations, when supplied and validated,
retain separate execution status.

> Decision: ADR-0049, ADR-0076, ADR-0086, ADR-0106

### §11.3 FastMCP contract

> Decision: ADR-0128, ADR-0025, ADR-0049, ADR-0057, ADR-0058, ADR-0059, ADR-0062, ADR-0063, ADR-0071, ADR-0073, ADR-0116

**Implemented native transport, 2026-10-06; actual journey outcomes are in the coordinator.** Rust owns the
finite request/response inventory, strict nominal inputs, scalar policies, classification and wire
failure meanings. Python registers model-derived schemas and serializes MCP envelopes. It does not
own another classifier. Native realization identity replaces the retired generation service; no
PostgreSQL fallback or compatibility reader is exposed.

Requests carry bounded parameters, result/byte controls and cancellation/deadlines. Enforce the
actual serialized response envelope, including JSON-RPC ID, resource URI, metadata and protocol
version fields. Structured recognized failures keep safe domain metadata where encoding remains
possible; otherwise fail once without recursive encoding or leaked credentials. Pinned original
sources/resources retain their realization and exact byte anchors. A 30-second overall deadline
includes decoding, embedding and native admission. Python owns cancelled workers until completion
and drains them before invalidating the native session. Local timeout is distinct from remote
read cancellation: residual immutable reads are bounded by the configured server query timeout.
The supported SDK stdio writer seam checks the actual final JSON-RPC bytes, including the newline,
and refuses an oversized packet before stdout emission. Pure serialization controls do
not establish persistent-store or operational MCP behavior.

### §11.4 Native serving and conditional workflows

**Implemented native runtime and consumers, 2026-10-06; operator activation not_run.** A managed local RocksDB SurrealDB server owns
persistent realizations; Rust SDK and ordinary native functions provide the first operation route.
Readiness, physical installation, executable sealing, publication and selection are distinct states.
Published exports and serving hydrate selected graph/projection inputs in coarse operations; complex
authoritative Rust kernels remain where appropriate. No startup hydration of unrelated relations is
required. Q0 checks actual persistent/native/MCP journeys; separately authorized Q1 covers fresh
pinned-library/live-vector reconstruction and operator activation.

Use sensible native query shapes, index/analyzer composition, result limits, query deadlines,
cancellation and server memory/cache settings. Do not claim an output limit bounds engine work or
invent a universal engine-step counter. Qualitative first-principles/library-informed design guides
these choices; quantitative speed/capacity claims require measurement. Custom APIs, WASM modules,
buckets, reactive enrichment and external exporters are activated only by a current consumer.
