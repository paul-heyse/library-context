## §10 Synthesis and briefs

S0 is the single writer of shared findings and programmatic assertions. Its independently known
native frame universe fixes the required C0, C1, C2, Structural, Analytic and Summary parents.
Each parent is a completed nominal invocation/frame under the exact selected input, context and
canonical definition. Optional NotRequested analyses remain distinct outcomes; a missing parent
is not fabricated as successful completion.

Model-owned pure operations prepare documentary conclusions, retain Summary facets, translate
qualified Structural/Analytic conclusions, emit findings/assertions, select configured and finite
automatic seeds, and render canonical briefs. `cpg-core::synthesis` acquires and publishes that
closure once. Replay reconstructs the same inputs and outputs; the source digest includes the
actual templates, original-code/setup operations and all deterministic emitters.

Mandatory catalog and retrieval construction covers public/evidence units independently of
brief selection. A seed without an admissible documentary Outcome produces `NoDocumentedOutcome`
omission while its catalog member and evidence remain available. Unrequested techniques or
missing optional embeddings do not erase the public universe.

**Implemented reuse, 2026-10-02.** S0 frame admission and automatic selection borrow one
`AnalyticParents` inventory containing exactly analytic frames, invocations and technique results.
Each operation retains its own declared inputs; seed replay constructs its independent parent
inventory. Actual consumed relations retain their acknowledged epochs through separate sessions;
sharing does not authorize an unfinished or different source universe.

> Decision: ADR-0049, ADR-0071, ADR-0078, ADR-0086, ADR-0106, ADR-0108, ADR-0116

<a id="section-10-1"></a>

### §10.1 Findings

A finding is a typed conclusion, never a sentence. `analysis::findings` owns its kind, nominal S0
invocation, exact subject, qualification, coverage and member/support digests. Members name their
roles; supports name Support or Scope and a nominal source. Typed derivations retain ordered
premise membership and source witnesses. Generated derivation views expose explanations without
becoming another writable authority.

Earlier owners publish qualified conclusions. S0 alone turns those into shared findings using the
common evidence floor/ceiling and qualification operation. Structural observations retain exact
source premises. Analytic grouping/ranking remains heuristic. A Summary finite value or complete
call question retains its earlier nominal derivation, channel, phase, scope and verdict. A checked
RefutedUnderModel proof becomes `BehavioralRefutation`; Unknown or proof=None remains a facet
without finding authority.

<a id="section-10-2"></a>

### §10.2 Assertions

`ProgrammaticAssertion` identifies its S0 invocation, public member frame, typed template/version,
kind, section, qualification, evidence status and deterministic text. Its support cites the exact
documentary, Structural, Analytic, Summary or authored-code source. Text is a rendering of those
records, never a source from which semantics are reconstructed.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| StructurallyObserved | Supported by admitted extracted/derived structural evidence |
| Documented | Authored official docstring, document or example applicability |
| StatisticallyDerived | A qualified heuristic result with its selected method/context |
| FixtureChecked | An explicit executed observation for its stated inputs/environment |
| Unresolved | The required evidence does not establish the assertion |

`analysis::policy` owns the kind→section/status policy and shared derivation rule. Producers,
publication and replay use it directly. Heuristic support may describe grouping, ordering,
Related entries or documentary similarity; it cannot assert a control, behavioral limit or
negative. Fixture status requires an actual task observation, not extraction success.
`BehavioralRefutation` belongs in Limits at the StructurallyObserved floor, preserving the
original checked Summary qualification and exact proof/support chain.

Control text retains exact A0 path/traversal/step, argument flow, literal binding, entry-value,
conditional-raise and unfollowed premises. Parameter names follow qualified signature/entity
links; literal and guard/raise text uses original source occurrences. Source and effective
signature options are distinct. An unproved correspondence cannot be supplied by their labels.

**Implemented premise rendering, 2026-10-03 (ADR-0120).** Summary assertion and brief text
consume the actual shared qualification. A nonempty basis is explicitly conditional and identifies
its premise set; an empty basis states that no additional typing/closed-world assumptions apply.
It does not remove the finite model, source-body entry, condition or scope. Conservative and
restricted alternatives remain separate, and fresh runtime source-binding admission refuses a
nonempty typing basis. Current packet/runtime acceptance is owned by the code-facts coordinator.

<a id="section-10-3"></a>

### §10.3 Brief structure and the Outcome order

One canonical brief is anchored to one selected public member and its admissible Outcome.
Sections retain their atomic assertion membership:

| Section | Sources |
|---|---|
| Outcome | Admitted authored docstring or exact C1-associated passage lead |
| Public access | Public exposures, aliases and qualified delegation conclusions |
| Applicable case | Admitted typed applicability/positive Summary or concept conclusion |
| Controls | Declared parameter/options and exact Structural control premises |
| Usage pattern | Authored source pattern, official scenario or admitted handoff |
| Limits | Boundaries, documented warnings and proof-backed behavioral refutations |
| Evidence | Exact support/source and qualification references |
| Related | Qualified optional community/concept/neighbor navigation |

Outcome order is the entry docstring's first sentence, then the lead sentence of an exact
member-associated documentary passage whose mention lies within that sentence. Otherwise the
brief is omitted explicitly. Whitespace-collapsed sentence display retains original byte ranges;
changelogs and nearest-by-vector passages never supply an Outcome. Documentation-only briefs
are explicitly marked, rather than pretending to have behavioral support.

Native Warning and ParamField templates require the exact C1 public-member association and
native component/attribute premises. ParamField uses the original lead span; Warning retains the
original inner span, literal title where available, and nearest ParamField label when applicable.
Option lookup keeps source/effective signature namespaces distinct. Unknown or ambiguous fields,
nested parameter fields, inline components, unavailable native evidence and foreign context retain
explicit component boundaries. Fenced/inline code cannot become component authority; forged or
out-of-container spans refuse. These are documentary applicability claims, not runtime controls
or negative proofs. A library using another document convention has no inferred component facts.

Configured and automatic seed decisions retain their own public slot, documentary summary,
official-usage/rank/community basis and selected/refused cause. Navigation scores cannot become
semantic support. The canonical document retains ordered assertion, documentary, Summary-facet
and code-boundary links and a content digest. Bounded UTF-8 parts are stored whole; an indivisible
part exceeding its declared bound refuses rather than silently truncating. Embedding token
admission is a separate later operation. Briefs are canonical unreviewed renderings.

<a id="section-10-4"></a>

### §10.4 Grounding checks

Shared model validators reconstruct the documentary, Summary, assertion, finding and brief
closure from completed nominal inputs. A coupled deletion of a source and its displayed output
cannot evade replay. Kind/status, member/context/scope, proof qualification, original bytes,
source membership, ordered parts and digests remain independently checked. Missing required
relations or corrupt bytes are refusal, not an empty valid brief.

Canonical source artifacts and chunks own original bytes. Prose slices authenticate their range,
content and source qualification; C1 association qualification remains distinct from native
artifact evidence. A later rendering never extends a completed provider vocabulary or invents
a source span. The unresolved/omitted inventory identifies evidence gaps without treating them
as negative behavior. Phase 5 structured and Markdown representations must hydrate the same
support closure.

<a id="section-10-5"></a>

### §10.5 Usage patterns

Authored patterns preserve original source occurrences and required setup. `source_code` admits
simple unindented materialized Python fences only after verifying the complete original and
materialized bytes. Original Markdown/MDX and extracted Python coordinates stay distinct;
every translated statement must be an actual occurrence of the admitted artifact. An
unmaterialized MDX/non-Python fence retains MissingMaterialization; transformed, unsupported or
foreign mappings retain their specific boundary.

`source_setup` closes the smallest admitted statement/dependency subset from exact import,
binding, execution and source premises. Setup, prior mutation, exceptions and context structure
cannot be removed to make an example appear runnable. Supported native call/property/constructor
patterns retain actual roles, arguments, defaults, source surface and constructor/reader
association. Structural extraction does not execute the snippet or prove temporal heap identity.
Unsupported patterns remain explicit. TaskReport observations, when supplied and validated,
retain separate execution status.

> Decision: ADR-0049, ADR-0076, ADR-0086, ADR-0106

### §11.1 Embedding spec and vectors

**Phase 4 Implemented; qualification in progress.** One immutable model-owned EmbeddingSpec and
exact value codec governs analytic and retrieval consumption. A use binds typed subject,
consumed text, input hash, spec, availability and the actual winning vector. Both consumption
owners use the realization adapter and retained immutable cache. Token admission precedes
service/cache effects; byte truncation is not token admission. Same spec/input uses in a
generation must retain identical winning bytes, including signed-zero representation.

Spec/value digests, dimensions, finite values and normalization are checked before use and by
cold stored-value replay. Corrupt required artifacts refuse; optional unavailability can yield
lexical-only retrieval without rewriting a completed analytic result. Live service behavior and
retrieval quality need separate evidence and are not claimed here. Phase 5 pgvector is a derived
physical realization of these canonical values.

> Decision: ADR-0106

<a id="section-11-2"></a>

### §11.2 Retrieval

**Phase 4 E0 Implemented; qualification in progress.** `domain::retrieval::build` renders typed
C0/C1 and completed S0 evidence into four families: ApiOptions, DocumentationDeployment, Scenario
and Source. Addressable contextual units retain input/context, typed origin, member/release
subjects, anchors and unit identity. Exact text/family deduplication keeps all contextual unit
occurrences. Canonical UTF-8 fragments retain their definition, ordinal and byte digest; the
renderer, membership and embedding-use closure share production/replay validators.

The mandatory family universe does not depend on selected briefs or vectors. Original evidence
uses the artifact/chunk owner; E0 introduces no second body store. RetrievalEmbeddingUse is a
later consumption event, distinct from AnalysisEmbeddingUse. Required corruption refuses and
optional vector absence remains lexical-only availability.

**Phase 5 query behavior is Implemented, 2026-10-02; real-library qualification and activation remain stopped.** Exact-name promotion, eligibility-before-ranking,
BM25, exact pgvector scoring, family-normalized RRF60, winning-unit witnesses, policy/channel
metadata and generation/request-bound cursors are serving contracts. A best fragment ranks its
unit; duplicate evidence cannot inflate family votes. Structured requirement witnesses are
independent of lexical/vector nomination. Addressable units and stored vectors alone do not
constitute a usable ranked service.

> Decision: ADR-0077, ADR-0078, ADR-0106

### §11.3 FastMCP contract

**Implemented in the Phase 5 working tree; qualification pending, 2026-10-02.** Rust owns request decoding,
semantic classification, complete nested packets and schemas derived from declared contracts.
Python FastMCP is a thin bounded transport/presentation adapter. It validates generated contracts
before effects and after final result assembly; no independently authored semantic Pydantic model
or Python predicate/cursor interpreter becomes authority.

The retained routes are `search_operations`, `find_operations`, `get_operation`, `browse_library`,
`get_evidence`, `search_evidence`, `compare_operations`, `search_capabilities`, `get_capability`
and `inspect_value_paths`. Their product responsibilities and packet structure are in
[§14.7–§14.9](api-and-evidence-product.md#section-14-7). Optional brief/native routes disclose
availability; `lookup_concepts` and a general `explain` remain separate Proposed capabilities.
Their former legacy implementation receipts do not qualify the replacement serving layer.

Startup must verify one published ready generation, frontier/profile, declared capabilities,
model/physical/schema identity, required relations and artifacts and selected embedding spec.
Each process retains its original pin. Missing required content or mismatched state fails
explicitly; there is no file fallback. SQLx pool/lease lifetimes and native worker/deadline
lifetimes are separately bounded. Cancellation retains an admitted native slot until completion.

Tools return schema-backed object packets and structured ToolResult output. Read-only,
idempotent annotations and protocol-only stdout remain required. Domain validation errors are
explicit tool/resource errors; internal details remain masked. The capability resource hydrates
the same support closure as the structured capability packet. Each assertion exposes its original
status, kind, qualification, text and nominal supports. Canonical BriefDocument bytes/digest remain
unchanged; resource presentation appends that model-owned assertion metadata, with its own wire
invalidation identity. Request and final SDK codecs execute with precharged scratch under the
original Rust execution grant and cumulative deadline. Response limiting middleware cannot
drop semantic structured output, and response caching cannot conceal degraded availability.

Discovery returns supported, unresolved and conflicting groups separately; strict mode returns
supported members. Missing evidence becomes contradiction only over complete relevant context.
Public member resolution retains ambiguity and separate source/effective contracts. Native
path inspection preserves the five verdicts, exact conditions, support closure and explicit
unexamined/truncated work. May-model compatibility is not proof of feasible execution or an
operation-wide absence claim.

**Code-facts expansion (Implemented; qualification in progress, 2026-10-03).** Signature packets
retain the explicit Source, EffectiveTyped and Synthesized roles and actual native signature
identity. Source return annotations and native effective ports remain separate. Generated typed
slots survive without invented source formals. Each exposed type retains its qualification,
required premise definitions and real native/source supports through the selected generation lease.

Typed structural facet values validate their evaluation domain before effects. Supported source
async/decorator, class/deprecation and raised-type questions consume attributed observations;
raised-type characterization is separate from finite runtime exception behavior. A matching
raised typing observation remains usable under partial coverage; a negative requires complete
native Types coverage for the same source artifact and analysis context, retained as a closure
witness. Missing raise traces stay unresolved. This correction is **Implemented**, 2026-10-03;
its scoped and assembled acceptance are owned by the code-facts coordinator §7. Decorator identity
selection is shared with analytics and follows normalized references before supported qualified-name
metadata. C2 persists canonical witness rows, and request-time classification refuses an unstored
citation. Incomplete metadata and unsupported runtime meanings remain Unknown or explicit refusal.
The coordinator's §7 records the bounded checks; full series qualification remains open.

**Implemented selected evidence and direct captures, 2026-10-03.** Original-source packets
retain bounded native diagnostics, pytest definition roles and usage observations under the same
source grant. Exact targets, arguments, receiver/applicability qualification and chosen/candidate
trace availability survive; unresolved associations and potential callback channels remain explicit.
These observations do not certify execution or extend a grant to a foreign source range.

Behavior packets expose the actual selected direct Summary capture contribution, including its
caller/callee frame, source-formal or literal value source and complete qualification/proof chain.
Source-declaration correspondence retains its actual report/native attribution separately from
native structural capture/timing/origin evidence. Timing candidates remain characterization,
and the value certificate remains conditional on entry to the bounded direct synchronous caller
frame. An empty capture list does not establish capture absence or transitive provenance.
Scoped original-evidence and capture PostgreSQL journeys are **Tested** in coordinator §7;
assembled same-tree adapter/Q0/full gates remain open.

**Implemented terminal questions, 2026-10-03; qualification pending.** S0 has a separate
TerminalSummary source for the exact direct following statement **given invocation entered**,
retaining its complete typing basis and StructurallyObserved status. The required nullable
assertion `terminal_question` field exposes the actual witness, frontier, normal-edge restriction,
target basis, scope, source qualifications and same-generation citation chain. Effects,
exceptions and cleanup remain unknown; no body execution or finite completion certificate
follows. Shared scope ownership validation preserves native Artifact qualification. A missing
question on an ordinary assertion is explicit null, without an old-format fallback.

These questions require the behavioral profile's Model/Summary stages through Catalog.
Default Catalog keeps those stages NotRequested. The code-facts coordinator §5/§7 owns the
independent source-scope finding and its scoped/assembled qualification.

Required signatures are indivisible. Packet/evidence row and byte budgets disclose omissions,
continuations or resource refusal; they do not silently shorten a signature. Cursors bind
generation, canonical request, section/group, policy and representation. Python lexical fusion
and Markdown presentation cannot change requirement outcome or evidence status.

**Incremental alignment (Implemented, 2026-10-02).** Generated discovery labels each numeric
code from its append-only codebook and exposes model route/predicate meanings. Numeric shape
and existing defaults remain unchanged. Typed packet output bindings compose direct reads,
child packets and startup preparation inputs; permission is checked before every attempted
canonical read, including empty ID lists. Immutable declaration metadata is shared. Canonical
proof membership and generation receipts remain separate admission obligations.

The model owns the four coarse failure categories and fixed public messages. After the actual
request ID is admitted, a recognized failure makes one complete pinned tool/resource envelope
attempt on the original grant. Tool failures use error-result metadata outside the success schema;
resource failures use JSON-RPC error data. Guard/deadline/budget refusal can prevent admission and
falls back once through the existing safe exception path. Driver text and configuration never
supply the public message. This does not promise universally bounded early framework errors.

### §11.4 PostgreSQL serving and conditional workflows

**Implemented Phase 5 contract, 2026-10-02; activation and real-library qualification remain
stopped.** The single generation store owns publication, selection and lease protocols. Serving views, grants and lookup
indexes are derived over those canonical relations; they do not copy records into another
semantic store. Generation readiness and operator selection remain separate transitions.
Readers retain leases, so cancellation, retirement, recovery and concurrent replacement must
preserve the pinned generation contract.

Exact vector retrieval remains the selected serving route. ANN, new PostgreSQL ranking engines,
trigram suggestions, new workflows or extra SQL libraries need a named consumer and scoped
qualification; library availability is not activation. Relation/schema corruption refuses rather
than silently returning fewer hits. Replacement serving state is reconstructed from current
canonical generations after validation, without old-format readers or rollback-only assets.

The [forward plan PostgreSQL workstream](../../plans/behavioral-model-forward-plan_2026-09-24.md#postgresql-workstream)
owns retained conditional workflow obligations; the cutover plan owns Phase 5 sequencing.
No current FastMCP pilot, live embedding or product differentiation claim is made by Phase 4.

> Decision: ADR-0078, ADR-0086
