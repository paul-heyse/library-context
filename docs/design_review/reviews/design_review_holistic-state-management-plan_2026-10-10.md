## 3. Contracts and consequential interactions

**Closure and assurance.** HS3 shares one immutable audit-local selection through verification, encoding/checksum and completed-state consumers. It explicitly preserves actual stored-row inspection, unexpected selected memberships/roles/aliases/originals and independent cold semantics. Shared selection therefore removes repeated preparation without making producer expectations their own oracle. A new family must declare recovery treatment, rather than merely join a table-name list.

**Audit terminality.** HS1 places the read owner before fallible asynchronous construction and retains it through success, semantic early exit, preparation failure and cancellation. Drain precedes pin release and client invalidation; cancellation transfers cleanup to an owned finalizer. This directly addresses `inspection.rs:157–224`, `compiler.rs:824–841` and compiler-owned stream drainage. It remains independent of capture sharing.

**Retirement and reactivation.** HS5 claims an exact invocation/item/incarnation, atomically nominates children while removing only the corresponding bounded outgoing holds, and finalizes after an empty proof. Both incoming and outgoing hold creation participate in retiring guards. Temporary exclusion prevents an old page from deleting recreated deterministic holds; post-finalization reactivation retains the permanent cutoff. Keeping installation coordination around short actual-effect transactions is a credible initial tradeoff.

**History and recovery.** HS6 separates selective live observation, permanent issuance fencing, rich outcome retention and destructive collection. Its amended maintenance-successor contract now reconciles this separation with protected incomplete cleanup/retirement. Era cut and deletion wait for migrated authorization and successor consumers; uncertainty retains protection.

**Selected backup.** HS7 uses one explicit transaction for selected content and comparison metadata, preserves selected anomalies for independent admission, and checks stream terminals, EOF and explicit cancellation. Data-only restore receives no runtime authority from imported records. Whole-service cold recovery remains a different operation.

## 4. Composition and execution fit

| Operation / question | Universe and method | Assurance, resources and output |
|---|---|---|
| Audit: is this exact publication internally valid? | Exact transitive closure; one owned capture, separately meaningful actual-state checks and canonical folding | Pin/read owner survives every tail; no closure-sized duplicate is justified merely by sharing |
| Native access: which selected rows are eligible or invalid? | Indexed membership/owner/source/document windows or bounded exact-ID batches; complete ordering/deduplication | Eligibility precedes quotas; late-window rows remain visible; streaming cannot hide upstream arrays or blocking sorts |
| Compiler: what complete graph result follows? | Existing declared projection, petgraph/finite kernels and selected settings | Complete universe and canonical order survive; release follows actual optional/cache-hit consumers |
| Ranked replay: what follows the issued cursor? | Immutable retained rows/template bound to session, snapshot, request, channels, digest and order | Service charges survive eviction while borrowed; request scratch uses selected limits; final envelope admission remains independent |

The physical routes address relevant growth dimensions rather than only returned row counts. Sparse preparation must avoid unrelated retained content; full integrity and global graph work may legitimately consume their complete selected universes. Retirement pages actual ownership effects, while maintenance collection pages eligible history only after permanent authority advances.

A long-lived backup transaction necessarily retains snapshot resources until cancellation. That cost purchases coherence; selected access removes unrelated payload transfer without replacing coherence with per-page snapshots. Last-use release and charged immutable borrowers similarly preserve useful sharing while reducing unnecessary simultaneous representations. No quantitative capacity or speed claim follows.

| High-degree retirement with late hold/reactivation — growth/failure | Claim/page/finalize guards reject conflicting attachment, preserve durable child nomination and exclude old invocations after reactivation. |

These changes have bounded ownership routes. Pure model/policy/kernel controls need no database; native concurrency and recovery cases appropriately require the real pinned service and explicit maintenance ownership.

## 7. Finding and foundation assessment

<a id="F01"></a>
### F01 — Era closure initially lacked authority to finish protected incomplete work

**Original diagnosis:** FP-04/05, DP-08/19/24, A2 and G5 were unresolved at the HS5/HS6 boundary. The initial §5.2 permanently refused old-era execution while retaining incomplete cleanup/retirement. §5.1 ordinarily resumed the original invocation, but no operation bridged that contract after era closure.

**Consequence:** a crash after a committed retirement page could leave an object indefinitely retiring, or encourage implementation to refresh the old request’s authority. Protecting records and gating deletion prevented unsafe collection but did not define legitimate completion.

**Amended target-only resolution, inspected2026-10-10:** holistic §5.2 lines205–215 defines a distinct admitted maintenance successor, original-effect reconciliation/fencing, atomic claim of remaining idempotent scope, original item/incarnation/cutoff, preserved completed pages and independently reconciled successive identities. HS5/HS6 gate post-cut recovery and era cut on working consumers. Unified §7.3 and coordinator §7.4/HS-F05 carry the same contract.

This supplies the missing Proposed operation without renewing uncertain old effects or reviving completed work. Static reinspection establishes the target correction only. Current disposition belongs in coordinator §8; implementation/recovery evidence remains part of HS-F03/HS-F05 closure.

Applicable foundations FP-01–FP-07 are **satisfied at Proposed target level**: ownership and propagation are coherent; contracts preserve consequential distinctions; operations compose without universal machinery; local controls remain possible; physical units follow selected data, actual effects and actual consumers. DP-01–12/15–24 and CI-01–11/13 are supported by the contracts above. DP-13/14 support existing-library composition rather than replacing justified domain guards with a generic framework. CI-12’s unchanged evaluator boundary is outside fresh assessment.

## 9. Alternatives and tradeoffs

| Alternative | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Preserve current routes and tune limits | Simpler immediately, but retains whole-array, whole-main, high-degree transaction and representation-lifetime amplification. Insufficient target. |
| Refine existing owners as proposed | Preferred: removes competing meaning and mismatched lifetimes while retaining exact content, independent admission and established service ownership. |
| Native selected-export adapter | Credible fallback if pinned metadata/planner composition fails; adds distribution/terminal ownership and requires an explicit scoped decision. |
| Closed immutable segments | Potential recovery/reuse benefit, but introduces layout and lifetime machinery. Justified only by an actual consumer or demonstrated selected-export limitation. |
| Maintenance-only history compaction | Simplest credible initial collector; explicit downtime/maintenance burden is appropriate for this deployment. Online compaction needs a concrete workflow requirement. |
| Universal state/workflow manager | No demonstrated missing capability warrants reinterpreting semantic, filesystem, SDK and recovery authority together. |

## 10. Verification and remaining uncertainty

Physical planner behavior, retirement transaction conflicts, issuance/outcome inventories, transaction-bound metadata/cancellation and post-era successors remain implementation qualification obligations. They are bounded decisions with named consumers, fallbacks and effect gates—not permission to ship an undefined or unsafe route.

Revealing controls should retain multi-window late corruption, complete-empty and negative domains, isolates/parallel relationships, delayed construction/tails, pre-intent issuance, unknown acknowledgements, same-content reactivation, interrupted successor/collector recovery, active ranking borrowers and selected-policy variation. Corruption fixtures must reach their intended independent validator.

No benchmark or additional evidence folder was necessary for this target judgment. HS11/UP9 retains one final-source acceptance boundary; BC3, SM8 and operator adoption retain their separate owners.
