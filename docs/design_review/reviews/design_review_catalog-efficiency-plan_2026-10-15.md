# Catalog efficiency: plan-set design review

**Design / target review — Accept scoped, 2026-10-15.**

The corrected five-document set is suitable as a coordinated investigation and selected-delivery
plan. It substantively covers all nine findings, eight investigations and four operator-approved
rule directions from the [source review](design_review_catalog-completion-efficiency-target_2026-10-15.md).
It preserves the useful semantic, ownership and fidelity foundations while allowing independent
corrections to proceed.

The initial draft placed final acceptance before target-state regeneration and obsolete-path
removal. Finding [F01](#F01) records that defect. The corrected sequence places authenticated
cutover before final acceptance and requires reconstruction and actual consumer checks against
the regenerated target. Static reinspection establishes that this plan defect is resolved.

This acceptance concerns the planning architecture. It does not accept the still-undecided native
protocols, establish implementation or runtime qualification, authorize execution, or close any
source-review finding or stopped-plan exit.

## 4. Composition, dependencies and execution fit

The decomposition enables useful independent progress. CD00 improves reliable cancellation of
later controls. CN01 can group transaction-local work while retaining the current attempt fence;
CN02 has its own narrower race contract. CA00 and CA01 are independent. Exact-ID lookup can
finish without changing pagination semantics.

CN00, CN03 and CN04 appropriately begin with consequential decision slices. Their replacement
mechanisms are not specified as already qualified. The outputs and failure cases are sufficiently
concrete to guide investigation, reject incomplete remedies and require delivery when a worthwhile
route is selected.

Conditional relationships are coherent. CN06 affects CC00/CC03 only if selector representation
changes. Broader readiness needs a legally earlier consumer rather than following automatically
from union optimization. Shared files require an integrator, without becoming runtime serialization barriers.

The corrected final sequence is:

~~~text
Selected deliveries and focused controls
                  ↓
CE80: authenticated cutover and obsolete-path removal
                  ↓
CE90: integrated review and target-state consumer acceptance
                  ↓
CE99: documentation handback
~~~

A retained investigation outcome must explain a credible conforming route and reopening condition.
An unsupported or deferred capability cannot silently close a mandatory finding. This prevents
investigation completion from becoming whole-track completion.

## 7. Stable finding and resolution

<a id="F01"></a>
### F01 — Final acceptance initially preceded consequential target-state cutover

**Medium priority · FP-02/03/05, DP-19/23/24 · A3/A4, G3/G5.**

The initial coordinator scheduled CE90 final integrated acceptance before CE99 regeneration and
obsolete-path removal. Native and compiler companions delegated changed-state regeneration to
CE99. A selected schema, selector or projection change could therefore mutate the actual target
after the evidence presented as final acceptance.

The consequence was an incomplete composition contract: pre-cutover controls could establish
local implementation while failing to establish reconstruction and consumer behavior on the
resulting installed target.

**Correction requested.** Perform required authenticated regeneration and obsolete-path removal
before final acceptance, or explicitly reopen acceptance after cutover. Preserve drainage,
independent reconstruction and real consumer checks. Handback must not introduce further target mutations.

**Resolution inspected, 2026-10-15.**

- Coordinator §5, lines 174–176, now requires CE80 before CE90 and makes CE99 handback-only.
- Coordinator §7, lines 238–248, requires reconstruction and real consumer controls against
  regenerated state and reopens affected acceptance after later mutations.
- Coordinator §7, lines 256–262, places regeneration and obsolete-path removal before final
  target-state acceptance.
- Native lines 74/288 and compiler line 202 now route cutover to CE80.

**Assessment:** resolved at Proposed/document-design strength. The correction removes the
ordering defect; actual cutover and verification remain not_run. The source review's F01 is
a different finding and remains owned by coordinator §6.

Current execution disposition, if subsequently scheduled, belongs to the coordinator's sole
disposition route. This report preserves the dated finding and its inspected documentary resolution.

## 8. Library fit and alternatives

The source review's exact native capability inquiry is an appropriate foundation because
production remained unchanged. The plans reuse its qualified boundaries and require exact-version
work where a new mechanism is selected.

The preferred immediate direction—correcting shared-native effects while retaining suitable
Rust/DataFusion kernels—is proportionate. Bulk insertion is not treated as eliminating per-record
engine work; database references are not treated as a complete admission protocol; unavailable
Arrow transport receives no adoption credit.

Local spill followed by independent native ingestion remains a credible larger alternative.
Factored selectors and postings must earn their construction, protection, cold reconstruction
and retirement costs. Current flat selectors, compact term arrays or existing caches may remain
where complete comparison supports them.

The plans also retain the simplest viable alternatives: removing duplicate preparation, carrying
one admitted request through retention, omitting an uneconomic cache, and retaining a justified
phase barrier. They introduce no universal scheduler, retry framework, proof cache or permanent run database.
