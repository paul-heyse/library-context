# Catalog execution and recurring efficiency patterns: target design review

**Decision: Revise.** Catalog compilation repeatedly turns overlapping logical views into complete
logical reconstructions and separately installed native member sets. Protected contributor-fragment
reuse is a useful correction, but it removes only part of this amplification. The best target is
operation-owned preparation over independently checked immutable contributions, exact logical view
selection, and physical materialization at boundaries that need independent ownership or useful
indexes. Preserve complete semantic identity and admission; change when and how they are realized.

This judgment rests on source-established unnecessary work, supported by the latest failed control's
phase evidence. It does **not** establish one dominant wall-clock cause, a SurrealDB engine defect,
or a measured speedup for the replacement. The timeout occurred before analysis, artifact admission,
publication and linked native query assertions. Those stages remain unqualified by that invocation.

The important distinctions already exist: semantic versus physical identity; completed contribution
versus admitted artifact; view selection versus publication; complete-empty versus absent; local
termination versus remote finality. They are strengths. The defect is their physical expansion:
each logical relation view earns another full closure digest, membership copy, seal, completion and
retention graph even while consumed only inside the same protected compilation operation.

Today `completed=true` is written before subsequent installed-view auditing
([compiler.rs:4765](../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L4765)). It is **not** sufficient
replacement authority. The proposed internal completed-input capability must first earn actual
membership/backing/original/alias completeness and protect exact incarnations. A descriptor, hash,
shared future or cache hit alone cannot stand in for that independently established protection.

The preferred target separates three operations:

1. **Complete a contribution:** independently establish its declared outputs, actual canonical
   content and supporting ownership through EOF/finality; retain exact selected physical protection.
   Check contributor-local alias incidence/endpoints and terminality here; establish reachability
   over the exact composed selected dependency closure, including valid cross-contributor chains.
2. **Select an internal view:** bind the protected contributions to a relation and dependency role;
   produce exact, collision-checked members or projected rows from shared preparation. Explicit empty
   declarations survive. No independent durable member set is implied by this semantic selection.
3. **Earn a transferred/retained/published boundary:** establish the exact composed inventory,
   necessary reader indexes, complete semantic identity and independent admission before atomic
   publication. A cold/imported owner independently validates actual content. Extra realization
   objects exist for actual ownership/access needs, rather than for every relation declaration.

This is a change to the physical completion contract. It must explicitly replace current per-view
completion consumers; it is not permission to bypass them while leaving their promises in place.

### F01 — Protected fragments still become fresh complete reconstructions

**Violated:** FP-07/A4; DP-10, supported by H5/H12/H14/H16/H19/H28.
`registered_or_install_views` loops through distinct views
([compiler.rs:1207](../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L1207)). Each live installation
invokes closure/membership/alias audit and logical roots
([live.rs:140](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/live.rs#L140)). The dirty correction
correctly caches terminal-validated normalized contributor fragments. However,
[audit.rs:2160](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/audit.rs#L2160) allocates six new
sorters; [2197](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/audit.rs#L2197) decodes and re-pushes
the cached rows; [2341](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/audit.rs#L2341) finishes,
serializes and hashes all families again. Overlap retains root-by-shared-content work.

The unreached final boundary repeats this pattern:
[earning.rs:259](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/completed_state/earning.rs#L259) cold-audits aliases,
prepares members and hashes each selection, then
[536](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/completed_state/earning.rs#L536) hashes the composed semantic
union again. Fixing only the live cache leaves admission/publication preparation amplified.

**Correction:** move full-state identity to actual consumption boundaries under F02's revised
contract; share immutable canonical runs and non-consuming ordered union/fold, reuse complete
family roots only for identical family dependencies. Independently transferred completions must
retain their closure identity or receive an explicit replacement codec/contract. The current v4
recipe remains a viable final identity; rows still need canonical order, exact collision detection,
explicit inventories and count. No concatenation of contributor digests is equivalent by default.

**Closure:** source inspection shows internal relation completion no longer performs full closure
identity work solely because another relation exists; final/cold paths use the intended lifetime.
Minimal revealing equal/unequal duplicates, overlap, empties, spill and late-terminal controls
compare canonical streams/roots with independent expected answers. No failed stream publishes
reusable preparation. A successful actual analysis/linked-native-query journey is later functional
acceptance, not a prerequisite to recognizing this defect.

### F02 — Internal relation distinctions impose independent native lifecycles

**Violated:** FP-01/FP-03/FP-07, A3/A4; DP-10/16, H1/H2/H8/H15/H23/H25/H27.
Completion builds singleton views for every declared output and cumulative views, then installs
the entire inventory ([compiler.rs:4778](../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L4778)).
[root_members:62](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/completed_state/earning.rs#L62) rescans contributions
and lowers root-specific member rows. [live.rs:249](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/live.rs#L249)
installs the realization, contributor holds, member windows, seal and completion. Actual member
verification scans/sorts again and checks two hold incidences per member
([earning.rs:106](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/completed_state/earning.rs#L106)).

Direct member addresses can improve sparse access and independent retention. That benefit does
not establish a need for every internal normalization relation to own a duplicate membership set
and full effect/recovery lifecycle. Empty relations also retain meaning without requiring the same
physical expansion as a separately published index. Growing the vocabulary multiplies machinery
even when useful content and independent participants barely change.

**Correction:** implement the three operations in §3. Internal consumers receive a protected
immutable-contribution capability, exact relation/contributor mappings and declared selection;
materialize indexes or member maps where their actual consumer warrants them. Keep boundary
ownership sufficient to retain backing and originals until all selected readers drain. This changes
physical schemas/receipts and requires an ADR/owner cutover, regeneration and removal of replaced
per-view installation paths after consumers migrate. Do not retain a parallel legacy realization.

**Closure:** internal dependency consumers operate through the new earned capability, including
complete-empty outputs, mixed contributors and cross-contributor aliases. Independent retained,
cold/imported, admitted and published consumers earn their own necessary authority. Incarnation
replacement, conflicting members, missing/off-page backing, cancellation and failed finality refuse
before reliance/publication. Source inspection identifies the native objects/effects removed and
the real purpose of each remaining materialization. No new global compile lock is needed.

### F03 — Set-shaped intent is lowered into scalar native assurance

**Violated:** FP-07/A4, DP-10; H7/H8/H18/H23/H26.
`inspect_expected_read` retrieves one row and guard
([held.rs:57](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/held.rs#L57)). Logical backing preparation calls
it per backing ID ([audit.rs:2091](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/audit.rs#L2091)).
Owned alias audit separately inspects alias, source hold, target and target hold
([audit.rs:1821](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installed_view/audit.rs#L1821)). Realization
verification adds scalar `require_hold` per member/endpoint as described in F02. Per-view closure
inspection also re-observes common descriptors before scoped reuse can apply.

These checks have real validity purposes. The defect is item-driven execution and loss of the
set-shaped input, not independent admission or CAS. Once F02 reduces required installations,
remaining inspection should use an operation-owned expected-record/hold window. The repository
already has indexed root windows and exact candidate hydration; this is a missing composition at
the assurance boundary, not evidence that native bulk retrieval is unavailable.

**Correction:** bulk retrieve exact requested record/guard/hold sets under the existing owned reader,
associate by identity, and retain per-record ExpectedRecord bytes/incarnations with charged
ownership. Reject missing required rows and extra, duplicate, inactive or mismatched results;
preserve optional discovery absence where it is the operation's contract. The receiving write
atomically checks the premises it relies on; a batch read does not authorize a later unchecked hold.
Carry protected immutable observations only through their real session/pin/epoch scope.

Native effects currently prepare expectations/capture issuance and execute intent, payload and
outcome-reference release ([authorization.rs:440](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L440),
[628](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L628)). This makes unnecessary effect
count costly. Remove/coarsen consumer effects first. Do not independently delete this protocol,
drop its fencing, infer conflict keys from digest logs, or add another retry owner.

**Closure:** batch/scalar revealing controls agree on complete correspondence and refusal classes,
including a late missing guard/hold and incarnation change before CAS. Input/delivery/retained
reservations, terminal drainage and independent outcome reporting stay owned. Source confirms
remaining loops issue true set queries, rather than a bulk wrapper around scalar calls.

## 5. Shared approaches and concrete contract boundaries

The common approach is **operation-owned preparation, exact consumer selection and scoped
assurance**. Several ordinary modules/functions are appropriate. One global proof cache, universal
workflow engine or common mutable registry would combine incompatible authority and lifetime scopes.

| Construct / owner | Input and output; complete dependencies | Validity, resource and terminal contract | Consumers and deliberate differences |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protected contribution preparation — compiler/native | Exact selected physical records/incarnations/full descriptors, declared outputs and interpretation version → immutable canonical fragments and relation membership preparation | Same protected installation/session/epoch and resource pool; independently validate actual members/backing/originals; publish preparation only after EOF/drain and final premise check; fail/cancel leaves no reusable success | Live internal completion and normalization. Cold/import admission independently observes actual state. Logical equality cannot choose another physical copy. |
| Non-consuming ordered union/fold — native ordered-run owner | Immutable already ordered runs under the same normalization/serialization recipe, `RecordId::Ord` key order and exact JSON row-byte equality → exact deduplicated ordered payloads, optional completed family root | Equal key/equal bytes dedup; unequal bytes conflict; charge merge heads, readers, buffers and output lifetime; complete every required input; failed/late terminal cannot yield a complete root | Live/frozen logical state and selected transport preparation where recipes coincide. Expose preserved bytes to avoid re-encoding; avoid decoding only where the prepared representation also supplies the validated ordering key. |
| Dependency-aligned family preparation — completion owner | Selected contributor incidence and normalization/model/codec version; explicit views, bindings and aliases separately → reusable family stream/root | Whole root reusable only for identical family dependencies; additions/removals, failed lookups and empties participate; retained preparations own reservations. Distinct resource owners do not borrow uncharged state. | Contributions/memberships/backing can share stable sets; view/binding/alias changes invalidate their own families. Cross-contributor alias reachability remains composed-set work. |
| Expected-record/hold windows — native control/reader | Exact pointers, required-versus-optional lookup intent and incidences → checked per-record bytes/incarnations/correspondence | Actual set query, not hidden scalar loop; required missing/extra/duplicate/inactive refusal; preserve optional discovery `None`; receiving CAS rechecks relevant premises; owned cancellation/delivery/drain | Alias/backing/realization audits and necessary final boundary checks; pure row inspection remains distinct from acquiring an effect-bearing hold. |
| Prepared lookup/adjacency — respective selection/restore owner | Immutable owned alias rows or decoded dump spool, identity/direction/duplicate policy → indexed lookup/frontier traversal | Resource-owned index and visited/frontier state; preserve cycles, multiplicity, missing evidence and source reachability; release after last borrower | Shared algorithmic shape, separate native and dump adapters. Selection and audit have distinct closure outcomes; restore retains independent admission. |
| Prepared partition positions — compiler execution | Exact hydrated union/topology plus partition membership/order → selected borrowed rows or gathered batches | Backing retained/charged until consumers finish; no omitted required intermediate; caller budget correspondence and failure states explicit | Independent validators/checkers retain their own state; rich output only where its consumer needs it. |
| Immutable recipe identities — Python adapter | One canonical Rust specification/version → one Rust response | Construction establishes immutable dependency; no ambient model/config lookup hidden in reuse | Encoder/projection/query/document properties; no service or native persistence authority involved. |

The first two constructs are enabling prerequisites for the highest-priority completion correction.
Native/dump/Arrow representations need not share one storage type. Reuse semantic recipes and
appropriate algorithmic primitives while retaining their separate trust transitions.
The family hasher consumes preserved JSON **row payloads**, not the spool's eight-byte length
prefix; retain the hasher's own tagging/count recipe. Current frame reading still decodes a row to
recover its ordering key. Removing that decoding requires a validated retained-key representation;
it is not automatically achieved by exposing bytes.

|---|---|

| More concurrency, larger budgets, longer deadlines or bare libtest alone | May change when a control terminates, but removes none of the established amplification. Not the architectural resolution. No arbitrary worker caps, raised limits or new retry framework proposed. |

`CompletedFamilyHasher::row` appends tagged canonical row bytes in order and then count
([completed_identity.rs:44](../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/completed_identity.rs#L44)); the
state identity is `native-completed-state/v4`. Whole-family reuse for identical dependencies and
ordered frame union can preserve that recipe. Contributor-digest concatenation, arbitrary Merkle
leaves or Arrow encoding cannot silently claim v4 equivalence. BLAKE3's internal tree structure
does not establish an application-level digest-composition operation for arbitrary contributor sets.

The preferred route has two complementary savings: **do fewer complete identity operations**, and
**compose remaining ordered input without sorting/serializing it again**. Even a correct merge
still reads/hashes the rows required by a flat identity. Do not claim it eliminates all work over
changed/overlapping sets. A new versioned identity is available if that residual work later has a
real consumer-driven justification; early design permits regeneration without legacy readers.

Important strengths to retain: selected physical mappings never collapse to global logical matches;
ExpectedRecord retains full bytes/incarnation; successful preparation waits for complete stream
validation; publication remains coherent and distinct from operator selection; original evidence
and attribution survive projections; resource admission precedes substantial allocation; reuse
belongs to its actual operation/owner. Independent import, cold validation and effect readback
cover new uncertainty and are **not** removed merely because their work resembles producer checks.

## 8. Similarity sweep coverage and non-findings

The sweep covered first-party native installation, selection, completion and control; compiler
provider/candidate/canonical preparation, transport/export/import and scoped consumers; restore;
workspace reuse; static embedding/cache and Python MCP adapters; selected environment/fixture/
verification/harness routes. Vendor/generated source and fixture data were not treated as runtime
recommendations. Docs publication tooling was excluded. This is a category-oriented source sweep,
not certification of every operation, analyzer, query/index plan or test.

Useful counterexamples constrain the shared design:

- [Embedding realization](../../../crates/cpg-core/src/embedding_realization.rs#L458) deduplicates
  before cache/service effects and shares pending requests across roots. No runtime embedding
  assessment was performed, and no need to replace this mechanism was established.
- [Native immutable cache](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/cache.rs#L203) readback/retry addresses
  winner/write uncertainty without repeating provider computation; resemblance to repetition is
  not a defect by itself.
- [Workspace composition](../../../crates/cpg-core/src/workspace.rs#L1284) preserves semantic aliases
  while deduplicating equal physical branches. This is the semantic/physical separation F02 needs.
- Restore already inventories descriptors once and uses an adjacency worklist; its remaining spool
  lookups are a bounded defect, not a reason to replace the whole restore architecture.
- Independent validators/checkers can require separate state and deterministic output sorting.
  F06 removes repeated union discovery, not independent semantics or required graph context.
- Development checks acquire new environment/fixture lifetimes. No justified recommendation to

  the physical route changes; a tiny synthetic fixture cannot replace its functional assertions.

No new blanket cache, mandatory profiler, gate, evidence registry, per-change cost model,

## 10. Priorities, prerequisites and remaining uncertainty

**First priority:** F01/F02 together. Decide the actual internal versus transferred completion
contract, enumerate its consumers and establish the earned protected-contribution capability;
then remove unnecessary per-view realizations and compose remaining canonical identity work.
F03 windows support the inspections that remain. F04 improves necessary alias closure. F05–F07
are distinct downstream corrections and must not be counted closed by a live-view patch. F08 has
low consequence and should not delay Catalog completion.

Prerequisite order differs from priority: the authority/identity contract must precede consumer
migration; shared non-consuming run composition and necessary lookup primitives can develop
independently once their semantics are settled. Independent cold/import/admission routes must
be updated before retiring replaced physical formats. Regenerate current project data/evidence
from pinned inputs only after exact live-owner drainage. No legacy runtime or second cache remains
as a comparison artifact.

No fresh product probe was necessary for this review: source and retained receipts settled the
