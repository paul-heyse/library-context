## 3. Composition, execution fit and alternatives

### Captured providers and diagnostics

`CompletedInputs::prepare_session` constructs each qualified provider once. An eligible plain alias
clones the exact already captured provider. Its ambiguity test still requires matching resolved
boundary and view identity; distinct physical/contextual capabilities are not globally coalesced.

The final corresponding correction in
[admission](../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/admission.rs) obtains the provider already
registered under the selected qualified name instead of calling the native provider factory again.
Qualified registrations and the existing `native_sources` collection remain unchanged. The first
selected binding and ambiguity predicate preserve the previous semantics. This is source-sound at
**Implemented** strength; compile evidence or a pure eligibility control cannot establish the new
native pointer-identity assertion or current full admission exit.

These are useful local corrections under DP-09/DP-10 and H12/H19. They preserve operational guards
and ordinary delayed-read checks. A global provider cache would add a broader validity and retention
problem without serving this alias operation.

Base, contextual nominal-closure, retrieval and installation observations use existing phase/tracing
mechanisms. Phase completion remains an observation of its actual awaited operation; dropped work
does not acquire fabricated terminality. These diagnostics localize uncertainty but do not themselves
establish the cause of earlier timeouts. No optional payload cache was configured in the examined
journeys, so `insert_window` retention batching was not selected as their remedy.

### Retirement

The new claim and finalization pages in
[retirement](../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/retirement.rs) reuse existing native windows and
owner-effect protocols. Every selected identity is checked again inside the effect. Scalar record
locks, the backup exclusion gate, lineage, incarnation, successor, item state and both hold directions
remain consequential premises.

All selected rows are classified before terminal mutation. Finalization projects only fields consumed
by `fn::native_incidence`, avoiding accumulation of compiler/original content bodies. Logical allowances
remain distinct from physical batching: claim and finalization consume their per-item units; child
nomination plus hold deletion consumes two units per edge. Newly nominated children invalidate
buffered discovery hints and return processing to the durable head.

This improves H2/H8/H23 execution grain without replacing the lifecycle model. More concurrent scalar
effects would retain repeated protocol overhead and contention on the shared backup gate. An unfenced
bulk delete would lose the required contract. The chosen bounded page is the simpler conforming
alternative.

### Required production, publication and independent assurance

Current [dispatcher composition](../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/dispatcher_completion.rs)
preserves nested completion metadata separately from acknowledged operation failure. Optional retention
can decline before native admission; a post-effect error is not suppressed by its Resource/Limit
label. Private singleton and capture work precedes the shared completion gate, while actual
contributor composition and coherent visibility remain inside it.

Independent receiving continues to earn authority rather than borrowing the producer's receipt.
The [pilot adapter](../../../scripts/catalog_pilot.py) independently leases pinned installed/source
bytes, checks declared source signatures and full-artifact BLAKE3, and binds native calls and
continuations to the same publication handle. These source properties support the intended route;
the actual pilot remains unestablished.

No measured speed, capacity or general benchmark claim follows from these changes or selected
control durations.
