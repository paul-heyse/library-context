# Catalog runtime efficiency plan-set target review

**Design · target · 2026-10-16**

**Decision: Accept scoped — the proposed plan set and its decision/implementation sequence.**

**Baseline:** `7a003677bd9ca4b738f27fcb80b27a0354391854`, plus the new plan documents and associated documentation updates inspected in the working tree. Production source is unchanged.

The plan set translates the runtime review into a coherent target without treating its proposed remedies as already proven replacements. It schedules the nine runtime findings, preserves the earlier efficiency work and its outstanding acceptance, and gives consequential open choices bounded decision routes. The combined design improves preparation, scheduling, native effect realization and resource lifetimes through existing owners.

## 1. Subject, standard and evidence boundary

The subject is the [coordinator](../../plans/catalog-runtime-efficiency-plan_2026-10-16.md) and its native, compiler, analysis and completion companions. They replace execution ownership of the remaining 2026-10-15 Catalog-efficiency scope while preserving historical evidence consumers.

The governing standard is [core/template 3.3, efficiency heuristics 1.0 and code-intelligence 1.5](../design_principles/standard.toml), including the repository binding. The functional target is the pinned API/evidence product and explicitly supported Behavioral analysis.

The workload premise includes complete pinned releases, many fact families, sparse and dense demand, overlapping closures, high-degree graphs, concurrent attempts and interrupted consumers. Sixty seconds remains an inquiry threshold, not an SLA.

This independent review inspected the complete proposed set, both source reviews, the previous efficiency coordinator’s decisions and acceptance boundary, relevant architectural owners and decisive production paths. Particular attention went to native request/reconciliation, actual incidence, receiving protection, closure preparation, producer polling, Base capture, Summary expansion and selected backup.

Evidence is static and dated 2026-10-16. No product build, test, probe, database query, service inspection or credential access ran. Historical receipts are assessed as documented evidence, not freshly verified executor state.

Embedding inference, MCP-submitted runtime queries, real-library qualification and operator adoption remain excluded. The review does not certify those capabilities or resume older paused tracks.

## 2. Responsibilities and semantic fidelity

The proposed dependency direction is sound. Model-owned operations determine meaning; compiler orchestration selects and integrates work; compiler-store earns exact inputs and independent admission; native capabilities own physical effects and protection; publisher owns coherent publication and selected backup. Existing run/service/storage owners retain recovery and resource disposal.

| Boundary | Governing meaning | Proposed realization and consumer obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Facts and completed inputs | Provider attribution, exact dependency views, role, coverage and logical identity | Demand may avoid unused acquisition; independent admission still checks its complete declared domain |
| Scope preparation | Root membership, ranking partitions, reverse traversal and negative universes | Native frontiers and reusable contextual preparation retain the semantically sufficient input universe |
| Base | Root/frame meaning, witnesses, physical/virtual distinction and stateful production order | Compatible immutable support may be shared; mutable production remains once per root |
| Summary | Proof identity, canonical states, residual qualification and deterministic partial meaning | Factor only genuinely equivalent work; distinct proofs and required output survive |
| Native effects | Exact request, era/generation, eligibility cuts, incarnations and disposition | Physical stages may change only after complete recovery and exclusion cases close |
| Receiving/publication | Fresh ownership, exact copy correspondence, complete closure and independent admission | Protected premises travel through preserving reads; a digest or producer success does not grant receiving authority |

These are operational contracts rather than vocabulary alone. For example, RN03 requires complete cross-page protection before publishing a usable receiver, and CX00 explicitly separates selective production from whole-domain admission. RA00 retains root-specific partitions rather than allowing a shared union to supply unrelated negative premises.

The plan therefore preserves model adequacy and authority at Proposed strength. It does not introduce a second semantic interpreter, shared admission cache or portable proof authority.

## 3. Composition, execution fit and revealing scenarios

The proposed remedies address different physical causes. Their common theme is to retain valid work and ownership at useful lifetimes; they do not require a universal execution construct.

**New fact family.** CX00 separates declarations from earned provider ports. An unused family no longer automatically creates native acquisition. Its controls preserve nearest/first ranking partitions, reverse edges, missing physical roots and incomplete coverage. This is a credible domain-extension route: new semantics belong in the model, while existing acquisition machinery follows reached demand.

**Independent ready producers.** CX01 changes execution placement while preserving one coordinator for readiness, result buffering, acceptance and ordered visibility. The inspected baseline stores ordinary producer futures in one `FuturesUnordered` polling task; synchronous `borrowed_cpu` suspends that task’s siblings. Separate joined tasks remove that dependency on the multi-thread realization without weakening semantic order.

The corrected plan also recognizes current-thread execution: `borrowed_cpu` runs inline there. Correctness and drainage remain supported, but sibling progress is not claimed unless fully owned joined blocking execution provides it. Explicit gated controls distinguish these properties.

**Native mechanism substitution.** RN00 compares the split protocol with atomic bounded apply/outcome. Its case table includes lost acknowledgements, absent-request fencing, delayed issue versus close/freeze, late collisions, reference-release failure, host loss and permanent-era compaction. The candidate changes physical realization without assuming that native atomicity discharges application recovery.

The existing exact absent-request reconciliation fence in `control/authorization.rs` is decisive: a delayed original apply must conflict with or refuse against the same request identity. The plan preserves that requirement rather than treating snapshot absence as noncommit.

**Equal immutable replay.** RN02 distinguishes equal bytes/equal holds from equal bytes/new holds. This matters because new ownership is a real lifecycle change even when canonical content is unchanged. Complete admission participation, retirement exclusion, parent transitions and revision consumers remain required. Narrowing writes does not become blanket read-only replay.

**Receiving and retained reads.** RN03 carries a complete operation-owned premise rather than repeating full assurance per window. Its controlled-write scope is explicit: privileged direct edits require quiescence and rebuilding affected state. Independent incoming-data admission, stale context/incarnation refusal and legitimate concurrent lifecycle protection remain intact.

**Overlapping or disjoint Base roots.** RA00 shares both discovery and typed reconstruction while preserving exact partitions and once-only mutable production. It retains singleton feasibility and allows shrinking only before mutable production. The plan does not retry Unknown native acquisition as a local Resource fallback.

**High-degree Summary expansion.** RA01 correctly distinguishes queue admission/pop allowance from candidate enumeration and residual construction. The inspected worklist does not promise to bound all such work. Same-residual and genuinely distinct-residual shapes provide useful settling evidence. Exact factoring, a credible necessary-output route, and an explicit model/support change are separate outcomes; none permits silent truncation.

**Backup with shared dependencies.** RP00 confines descriptor reuse to one exact snapshot. RP01 ends the exact transaction/session after checked source EOF, before local carrier work. Together they remove repeated acquisition and excessive lifetime without weakening selected closure, physical copies or independent restore.

These routes support FP-07 at the plan level. Actual native protocol safety, resource behavior and high-degree Summary feasibility remain implementation or investigation obligations, not accepted production claims.

## 4. Library fit and complete alternatives

The plan makes appropriate use of existing capabilities rather than selecting features for their own sake.

- Native atomic transactions are a candidate for a bounded effect, not justification for a whole-compilation transaction or removal of recovery.
- Bulk insertion and qualified scalar locking remain useful. Record-ID-array queries are not assumed to provide equivalent exclusion.
- Native indexed frontiers suit selective persisted demand. DataFusion remains suitable for set joins, ordering and spillable preparation where the full semantic universe is required.
- Existing SCC schedules, indexed proof buckets, delta queues and Pareto representatives remain the Summary foundation. No evidence justifies replacing them with another graph engine.
- Operation-local maps and typed union/partition views are sufficient reuse mechanisms. Global adjacency caches, permanent certificates and executor frameworks would add unnecessary ownership and invalidation machinery.

Compared with retaining the current physical paths, the proposal removes identifiable repeated work. Compared with larger batches alone, it addresses server stages and shared writes as well as crossings. Compared with broad transaction fusion, it retains bounded failure and retry units. Compared with aggressive semantic narrowing, it preserves negative universes and distinct evidence.

No quantitative speed or memory benefit is established. That limitation does not negate the source-established amplification or require a benchmark campaign before selecting a better structure.

## 6. Findings and authoring corrections

**No open material findings remain against the corrected plan set.** Original runtime and efficiency findings retain their source IDs and current disposition at [coordinator §6](../../plans/catalog-runtime-efficiency-plan_2026-10-16.md#6-sole-disposition-and-current-checkpoint). This review creates no replacement production finding ledger.

Two bounded authoring clarifications were made during review and inspected afterward:

1. **RC04 decision route.** The initial route unnecessarily suggested succeeding ADR-0168’s execution-placement choice. ADR-0168 owns suitable exact-selection, coordinator and visibility decisions; it does not require unspawned futures. The corrected plan calls for a new placement ADR and semantic §15.1 update, superseding an existing clause only if a concrete incompatible clause is identified.
2. **Runtime-flavor support.** The corrected compiler companion distinguishes multi-thread sibling progress from current-thread correctness/drainage, and adds separate controls. This prevents a general progress claim from being inferred from independent tasks alone.

These are documentary corrections. They close no production finding and establish no Tested runtime behavior.

FP-01–FP-06 are satisfied within the proposed ownership and contract scope. FP-07 is satisfied for the credible proposed routes and conditional sequencing, while production correction remains open. DP-03/09/19/20 and CI-04/05/08/11/13 constrain the decisions and their eventual acceptance rather than being waived by efficiency goals.

## 8. Verification, uncertainty and rule impacts

Implementation verification is proportionate: minimal revealing controls during delivery, actual pinned-native evidence for persistence/conflict/finality, then integrated review and selected inherited functional acceptance on the final target. RE80 precedes final acceptance when state changes; RE99 is documentation-only. Later production mutation reopens affected acceptance.

Production builds/tests, runtime qualification and performance measurement for this review: **not_run**, deliberately; static document/source inspection supplies the scoped judgment.
