# Algorithms and library fit for catalog creation

Supporting inquiry for the principal catalog-creation review, reached through the coordinator-owned [evidence index](README.md). Examined 2026-10-14 at `b0306d62`. This document supports the coordinator's architectural judgment; it is not a separate overall verdict or disposition ledger. Source observations below are **Implemented**, library contracts are **Interface-checked**, and recommendations are **Proposed**. No latency, memory-use measurement or assembled qualification is claimed.

Scope: projection materialization, structural delegation/controls/handoffs, optional analytics, finite summary scheduling/worklists and their cpg-core prepared/scoped consumers, for both Catalog and Behavioral profiles. Catalog completion latency is the priority; Behavioral-only work is identified separately. The governing assessment is core 3.3, its efficient-architecture heuristics, and code-intelligence 1.5, especially FP-07, DP-09/10/12/13/15 and CI-03–09. The coordinator owns integration with the wider product and lifecycle assessment.

## Synthesis

The implementation already has useful reusable topology and semantic progress mechanisms. It does not need a blanket graph-engine replacement, a second generic workflow engine or an unqualified conversion to relational execution. Its strongest opportunities are narrower: indexed correspondence inside already scoped frames; compact witness storage; sharing stable classification and adjacency work; and keeping optional enrichment's combinatorial work demand-driven. Preserve the existing SCC order, semantic Pareto frontier, condition admission and unknown outcomes.

Three concrete causes deserve the principal review's attention:

| Evidence key | Cause and consequence | Scope and proposed owner |
|---|---|---|
| ALG-01 | Scoped frame kernels still repeatedly scan relations for keyed correspondence. Work grows with both selected entities and relation size, after frame closure has already been prepared. | Structural build and analytic attribute producers; applies to Catalog and requested analytics. |
| ALG-02 | Structural witnesses expand shared prefixes into vectors/step records; Behavioral controls additionally rescan all argument flows for each visited semantic state and retain copied pending prefixes. | Structural traversal/witness owner. Catalog delegation and Behavioral controls have different contracts; do not merge them into a generic reachability answer. |
| ALG-03 | Optional analytic layers deliberately generate pair sets and concept contexts whose size can grow sharply with degree and independent attributes. Demand gating is present; honest caps do not establish fit for enabled large frames. | Analytics policy and consumers. No finding that these methods are mandatory for every Catalog compilation. |

These are source-grounded amplification mechanisms, not measured dominant bottlenecks. Their effect relative to extraction, native persistence, normalization and publication is outside this inquiry.

## Existing strengths and preservation constraints

`MaterializedGraph` uses `petgraph::Graph<EntityRef, ArcId, Directed, u32>`, not a pair-keyed simple graph ([snapshot.rs:21](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/projection/snapshot.rs#L21)). Construction inserts the vertex universe independently, then every arc with its identity; hydration checks canonical entity and arc order and creates incoming/outgoing ArcId indexes ([snapshot.rs:104](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/projection/snapshot.rs#L104), [snapshot.rs:165](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/projection/snapshot.rs#L165)). Thus isolates and parallel relationship evidence already survive. `reachable` traverses the complete named universe before applying the output selector, using petgraph `Dfs`, `EdgeFiltered` and `Reversed` ([snapshot.rs:221](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/projection/snapshot.rs#L221)). An optimization must preserve this distinction between answer selection and computation universe.

`PreparedGraphs` loads only selected projection metadata, streams selected chunks, drops encoded chunks after hydration, and retains one graph per selected projection ([analysis_graphs.rs:99](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/analysis_graphs.rs#L99), [analysis_graphs.rs:121](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/analysis_graphs.rs#L121)). It prepares an invocation SCC schedule only for Behavioral and releases graphs after their last possible consumer ([analysis_graphs.rs:72](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/analysis_graphs.rs#L72), [analysis_graphs.rs:167](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/analysis_graphs.rs#L167)). The compiler declares Summary's Behavioral invocation projection separately from Structural's invocation and definition projections, and avoids preparing Analytic graphs when analytics are unrequested ([compilation.rs:198](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/compilation.rs#L198), [compilation.rs:901](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/compilation.rs#L901)). Recommending “prepare graphs once” as missing would misdescribe the baseline.

Structural consumers prepare one compact closure plan, then load and discard one parent grain at a time ([analytical_scopes.rs:88](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/analytical_scopes.rs#L88), [structural.rs:133](../../../../crates/cpg-core/src/structural.rs#L133)). This limits rich evidence lifetime without shrinking the graph universe. Ordinary local indexes inside a grain can improve it without replacing that owner.

`PreparedDelegation` owns frame adjacency and unresolved-event buckets; each visited node validates and orders its complete adjacency once, including caching failure ([delegation.rs:280](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L280), [delegation.rs:369](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L369)). Structural production shares that preparation across public seeds ([build.rs:349](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/build.rs#L349)). It is bounded breadth-first traversal, not unrestricted path enumeration: vertices are queued once, a single canonical parent is recorded, final arcs are selected by shortest depth, and retained witness count is capped ([delegation.rs:460](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L460), [delegation.rs:510](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L510)). Cycles therefore do not imply exponential delegation path exploration.

## ALG-01: indexes and relational selection before another graph algorithm

Structural scope construction scans modules for each callable source and artifact uses for each source callable ([build.rs:162](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/build.rs#L162)). Public-slot construction scans core links per member, and configured-seed counts repeatedly scan public candidates ([build.rs:207](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/build.rs#L207), [build.rs:279](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/build.rs#L279)). These operations ask equality/existence/grouping questions, not graph questions. The frame is already scoped; scoping alone does not eliminate the repeated scans.

Requested concept attribute production similarly scans symbol resolutions to resolve a symbol and scans type members/slots by sequence/list ([attributes.rs:141](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L141)); RCA tests target membership by scanning structural scope per arc ([attributes.rs:589](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L589)). Each namespace independently calls `facts`, constructs incidence and builds a bitset context ([attributes.rs:703](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L703)). Native metadata symbol resolution also uses a repeated `any` scan ([native_attributes.rs:87](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/native_attributes.rs#L87)). Behavioral handoffs iterate bindings and scan alternatives to find a producer at the corresponding occurrence ([handoffs.rs:605](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/handoffs.rs#L605), [handoffs.rs:707](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/handoffs.rs#L707)). These are concrete candidates, not an assertion that every kernel is unindexed.

**Proposed correction.** Build charged frame-local buckets for source→modules/roles, member→applicable links, scope membership, occurrence→event alternatives and symbol/sequence→all evidence rows. Keep all competing rows and apply existing ambiguity checks; an index must not select an arbitrary first match. Count configured slots in one pass. Share stable native attribute correspondence across FCA/RCA namespace calls, retaining each namespace's object universe and provenance separately. Owners remain model operations; physical preparation can sit beside their invocation-local data.

**Alternative: DataFusion bulk joins.** When these inputs are still columnar, relational semi-joins, joins and group aggregates can perform the correspondence before rich decoding and expose filter/projection decisions to the optimizer. The existing scoped loader already uses this route for selection. DataFusion is preferable when many rows are consumed in bulk and the result is a keyed selection or aggregation, rather than repeated row lookups. A small charged map is simpler when the rich rows are already required and the frame is modest. Avoid converting already prepared native graph adjacency back into tables merely to implement BFS/SCC; repeated plan/stream setup for each edge or proof would add crossings.

DataFusion does not decide domain cardinality: duplicate provenance, nullable unresolved endpoints, ambiguity and context equality must remain explicit. A join can still produce a necessary or accidental Cartesian product. Check all join keys against input/context/role identity, project only necessary columns, and preserve unmatched assessment rows rather than silently turning unknown into absent. Streaming the output does not imply joins or sorts have no retained state. Closure evidence should compare attributed outputs and ambiguity/refusal outcomes under a frame with duplicate module candidates, two roles, foreign contexts, and unresolved targets; inspect the resulting physical plan if choosing the relational alternative.

## ALG-02: compact witnesses and shared traversal work

For Catalog delegation, shared checked adjacency is already present, but each public seed receives its own traversal state. Repeated public wrappers that reconverge on the same implementation revisit that suffix for each seed. Within a seed, `path_to` walks a parent chain and creates a vector for every selected witness and unresolved event ([delegation.rs:496](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L496), [delegation.rs:539](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L539), [delegation.rs:578](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L578)); Structural then emits each path's steps separately ([build.rs:390](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/build.rs#L390)). Shared prefixes therefore become repeated storage/output work, even though search itself is bounded. Boundary classification also rereads source-role facts per traversed edge ([delegation.rs:217](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L217), [delegation.rs:481](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analysis/delegation.rs#L481)). A frame-local classification cache is lower-risk than caching whole seed answers.

Behavioral controls use semantic visited keys `(target, formal, suppressed)`, so they are also finite-state rather than all-path search ([controls.rs:635](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/controls.rs#L635)). However they scan the whole argument-flow relation for each expanded state, clone its prefix for every outgoing edge, and retain pending entries until traversal ends, including entries later skipped as visited ([controls.rs:663](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/controls.rs#L663), [controls.rs:691](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/controls.rs#L691)). Reconvergence and cycles add duplicate pending prefixes before admission. The semantic visited set prevents unlimited expansion, but path bytes still follow admitted outgoing work times path length. This is a stronger avoidable amplification than the existence of BFS itself. Controls are requested through Local predecessor status, with uniform unrequested Local yielding false ([frames.rs:76](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/structural/frames.rs#L76)); do not attribute this Behavioral cost to ordinary Catalog compilation.

**Proposed correction.** Index argument flows by `(caller, source formal)` and unfollowed arguments by the same semantic key. Store a predecessor/witness arena and queue handles, materializing a bounded path only where publication requires it. Keep at most the canonical currently relevant pending state per semantic key when that preserves the existing BFS order and bounds. The authoritative witness contract must specify whether it retains one canonical path, shortest alternatives, or all distinct proof alternatives; the current control visited key already merges path histories. Do not silently broaden or narrow that contract while changing storage.

For many public seeds, compare independent BFS with source-labelled frontier propagation or shared suffix reachability. Only adopt sharing if the key includes exact materialization/frame, boundaries, direction/arc roles and bounds, and per-seed partial/truncation outcomes remain equivalent. SCC condensation can share Boolean reachability on the named invocation relation, but cannot directly supply per-seed shortest paths or semantic controls, and cannot conflate definition containment with invocation. Compact bitsets can represent visited/source sets over a complete ordinal universe; persistent evidence must retain ArcIds and map ordinals through the exact graph owner. An all-pairs closure is usually the wrong replacement: it can create a dense retained result that the bounded public-seed consumer never requested.

**Closure scenarios.** Use a chain with many public aliases to one suffix, a branching diamond with parallel call alternatives and distinct qualifiers, a self-cycle plus a two-node cycle, an external/subsystem boundary, and an unresolved event. Compare chosen witnesses, omitted flags, examined work and partial reasons. For controls, include reconverging suppressed/unsuppressed states and two different formals at one target. A compact witness must not erase evidence identity or incorrectly merge those states. No new probe was run; a pure in-memory diamond-and-cycle fixture comparing old/new outputs is the minimum revealing probe if implementing this remedy.

## Behavioral summaries: retain the SCC and delta design, specialize candidate indexes

Summary scheduling uses petgraph `kosaraju_scc`, canonicalizes members and produces callee-first condensation order with deterministic ties ([summary_schedule.rs:34](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_schedule.rs#L34), [summary_schedule.rs:75](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_schedule.rs#L75)). The schedule binds to exact materialization identity, not merely nominal graph key. Known conservative invocation topology is explicitly distinct from complete dispatch knowledge. SCC order is a dependency schedule; it does not establish a semantic fixpoint.

The semantic fixpoint is implemented separately. `Progress` indexes branches by owner, keys frontier admission by semantic branch identity, and retains proofs separately ([summary_production.rs:1134](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_production.rs#L1134)). The Pareto frontier suppresses dominated/equal-cost reopening while preserving admitted evidence; pair queue deduplicates pending work and charges explicit admission/processing units ([summary_worklist.rs:87](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_worklist.rs#L87), [summary_worklist.rs:168](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_worklist.rs#L168)). `Discovery` builds caller/callee and support indexes once. New branches generate pairs only through incident call buckets, including self-calls once, rather than rescanning every call ([summary_production.rs:1210](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_production.rs#L1210), [summary_production.rs:1467](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_production.rs#L1467)). Component processing retains processed pairs, rejects stale representatives and records residuals on work exhaustion ([summary_production.rs:2157](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_production.rs#L2157)). This is already a delta/worklist design.

Remaining candidate growth is visible where each new branch pairs with all current other-owner representatives for an incident call, and where path candidates combine several support buckets ([summary_production.rs:1498](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_production.rs#L1498), [summary_production.rs:1327](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/execution/summary_production.rs#L1327)). A more selective descriptor/port index could reject structurally impossible compositions earlier. Preserve all qualifying support alternatives; genuine proof combinations cannot be removed by arbitrary endpoint deduplication. Compare compatibility-index filtering against the existing endpoint buckets before introducing Datalog or another incremental runtime. Needed closure case: recursive SCC, two Pareto-incomparable proof costs, equal-cost independent evidence, and a late improved callee proof. The resulting semantic states, finite witnesses and explicit residuals must agree.

Condition handling already uses `biodivine-lib-bdd`: bounded apply preflights operand-pair work and calls `binary_op_with_limit` ([kernel.rs:106](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/conditions/kernel.rs#L106)). `PreparedConditions` memoizes lowered diagrams by root identity over one exact admitted node inventory ([preparation.rs:6](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/conditions/preparation.rs#L6)). Thus “replace paths with BDDs” is not a complete remedy: BDDs compress Boolean conditions, not relationship evidence or proof alternatives. A shared condition-operation cache could be considered only for demonstrated repeated operand/operator pairs with complete model/atom dependencies and bounded retained lifetime. Do not convert refusal into false; do not equate graph cyclicity with Boolean or transfer convergence.

## ALG-03: optional analytics and their honest complexity

Analytics selects PageRank, communities, FCA and RCA independently and narrows demanded inputs by technique/layer ([build.rs:29](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/build.rs#L29), [build.rs:208](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/build.rs#L208)). Preserve this positive demand boundary. An explicit unrequested result remains useful; it is not a reason to compute the technique anyway.

- **Ranking:** current kernel sorts and combines weighted pairs once, then performs sparse edge flow plus vertex updates each iteration, retaining dangling-node treatment, residual and work-limit outcomes ([ranking.rs:108](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/ranking.rs#L108), [ranking.rs:164](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/ranking.rs#L164)). It computes over scope, then publishes scores; filtering to public APIs first would alter global scores. Its iteration work follows the vertices and aggregated arcs, not every vertex pair.
- **Communities:** co-use generates unordered pairs among distinct targets per usage owner, after a squared-degree work preflight ([build.rs:570](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/build.rs#L570)). Similar type/mention layer pairing exists ([attributes.rs:265](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L265), [attributes.rs:380](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L380)). If full pair evidence is required, the quadratic output is inherent; a hash map cannot remove it. Compare retaining owner→targets incidence and deriving aggregates/evidence on demand before emitting every contribution. A weighted incidence formulation may avoid explicit clique materialization, but is a different optimization requiring equivalent weights and evidence lookup, not a drop-in Leiden call.
- **Leiden:** uses an existing library, one combined weighted graph and repeated independent runs. The retained policy has four resolutions, ten seeds and up to 100 iterations, and the implementation computes ARI/NMI across seed pairs at each resolution ([policy.rs:63](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/policy.rs#L63), [communities.rs:122](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/communities.rs#L122)). Those runs serve stability diagnostics; reducing them changes the analytic contract. Community partitions are heuristic suggestions, never correctness partitions for summary/control propagation. Assess selected recipe and consumer need separately from Catalog's mandatory path.
- **FCA/RCA:** contexts retain both bitset rows and columns; closure intersects selected rows/columns, then bounded lectic concept/pseudo-intent enumeration repeatedly closes against the growing implication basis ([concepts.rs:59](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/concepts.rs#L59), [concepts.rs:112](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/concepts.rs#L112), [concepts.rs:161](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/concepts.rs#L161), [concepts.rs:228](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/concepts.rs#L228)). Many independent attributes can produce many concepts. The examined-state cap preserves derived exact results while marking enumeration incomplete; it does not turn whole-lattice work into a generally cheap task. Current RCA adds direct Calls/Handoff attributes and invokes the same context analysis once per namespace ([attributes.rs:589](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L589), [attributes.rs:703](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/attributes.rs#L703)); policy explicitly names `one-RCA-step` ([policy.rs:77](../../../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/analytics/policy.rs#L77)). Do not describe it as an unbounded relational fixed-point engine. If the consumer wants a particular capability combination, compare direct intent/extent or implication queries against enumerating every frequent concept. That changes requested output semantics and requires an explicit consumer decision.
