## Priority, ordering and preserved strengths

Selective ID reads, warm attachment, missing provider reuse and high-degree Structural work
have the strongest consequences in this slice. Dependency order differs from priority:
capture-key/lifetime contracts enable sound reuse, but selective access and provider-local
preparation can proceed independently. O-F18 and O-F19 are related but distinct; O-F23,
O-F24 and O-F25 likewise have separate closure conditions.

Preserve admitted-source/spec checks, exact selected membership including absence/virtual
roots, full-value collision checks, explicit partial/refusal states, model-owned coverage,
charged bounded lifetimes and independent public-byte evaluation. Useful existing library
composition includes DataFusion provider-controlled selection/projection, Pyrefly's joined
pool, Ruff's observation-aware checker and BDD effective-support canonicalization.
