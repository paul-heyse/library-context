# Consumer coverage and selection ledger

**Proposed selection, 2026-10-03; coordinator-owned.** Baseline `main700476d9` with preserved serving edits. This ledger completes the target-consumer dispositions for the finite supply inventory. It is evidence for the [principal review](../../reviews/design_review_code-facts-expanded-target_2026-10-03.md), not architectural authority or an execution register.

Each provider-fact record inherits its concept route below; the [supply inventory](coverage-inventory.json) retains exact fact IDs, surfaces, competing providers and limitations. Generic supply candidates are refined here: retained primitives are not all independently proven consumed in the tree, and absence of a new selected relation is not an upstream-absence claim. Current decisive consumer/source evidence and twelve detailed operations are in the principal review. The source worker’s 41-payload shortlist remains a candidate list, not a storage requirement. C12 deliberately refines diagnostics from an offline-only lead to bounded original-evidence corroboration; it does not require a new diagnostic subsystem.

Every row has a route, decision and limiting reason. Repeat provider implementations of the same concept are alternatives or attributed corroboration, not duplicate semantic authority. “Retain” means preserve the existing responsibility/model rather than certify every upstream subpayload as consumed. Future admission requires a named output difference and the card’s basis/coverage controls.
