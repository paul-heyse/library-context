# Native effects and lifetimes

Bounded supporting inquiry for the [principal target review](../../reviews/design_review_catalog-publication-performance-target_2026-10-17.md), which owns the combined judgment and finding disposition. This note creates neither an enclosing verdict nor a second execution ledger.

**Examined baseline:** `7f353fa1fffd081eb9f25003b61cbea9f74ded67`, 2026-10-17. Production sources below were inspected directly. The inquiry was read-only; no builds, tests, probes, service operations or configuration inspection ran. Concurrent documentation work was preserved. Diagnoses below are source-established structural observations; remedies are **Proposed**, with no measured speedup or individual attribution to the recursive timeout.

The governing standard is core3.3, its efficient-architecture companion1.0 and code-intelligence profile1.5, loaded through [standard.toml](../../design_principles/standard.toml). The relevant lenses are operation-shaped execution, complete physical expansion, immutable-property reuse, independent assurance, exclusion scope and proportionate recovery. The [storage owner](../../../design/sections/storage-and-publication.md) explains the existing mechanisms; those mechanisms do not constrain the target alternatives.

## 1. Expanded records and per-record coordination before bulk mutation

<a id="NI-F01"></a>
**NI-F01 — Implemented structural amplification.** Compiler ingress constructs physical payloads, memberships, optional graph anchors/endpoints and aliases, then installs nodes, aliases and memberships as separate sequential window streams. See [compiler.rs:5898](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L5898), [6048](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L6048), [6096](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L6096) and [6134](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L6134).

An ordinary fresh non-graph logical row normally creates a payload and membership, contribution-to-record holds for each, physical guards and incidence predicates. Graph rows add more structure. Exact counts vary with equality, endpoint reuse and record kind. The logical transfer row count therefore does not describe native write cardinality. [ImmutableWindow::unit](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/immutable_window.rs#L14) and [hold_plan_in](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control.rs#L604) establish the expansion.

The installed [native_immutable_install](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql#L229) performs bulk mutations only after several other passes:

- Each row and hold receives a scalar existing-record SELECT into `$prepared`.
- Declared incidence and changed incidence are derived separately.
- Affected predicates and deferred guards receive scalar locked observations.
- Contribution/completion parents are checked.
- Distinct hold owners and objects receive scalar existence checks.
- New ordinary rows, relations, originals and holds are then inserted in groups.

The deferred `array::find` at line256 adds repeated lookup within this larger operation. Replacing it alone leaves the expanded records and other scalar work. New incidence also changes shared contribution/endpoint predicates and sometimes physical guards per window; attempt stripes do not remove these shared write addresses. Conversely, shared owner `FOR UPDATE` reads alone do not prove serialization: the [storage owner](../../../design/sections/storage-and-publication.md#section-6-2) records the pinned realization's disjoint-stripe behavior.

Index maintenance belongs in this assessment. [Installation declarations](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installation.rs#L48) give memberships three access orders; [control schema](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql#L49) gives holds both directions; [canonical schema](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/schema.rs#L154) gives graph payloads anchor, semantic-key and multi-valued scope indexes and aliases two directions. [Scope construction](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/adapter.rs#L123) produces keys from declared reference fields. These indexes have real consumers, including [selected membership access](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/selection.rs#L1269), [node-qualified membership](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/selection.rs#L1426), audit and retirement. Their cost is unmeasured; this is not a recommendation to drop them without replacing their access obligations.

**Target and alternatives — Proposed.** Prepare one complete immutable installation packet with semantic distinctions, exact physical expectations and lifecycle incidence. Compare fusion of compatible streams under the existing representation with immutable contribution extents protected through authoritative roots. A larger alternative derives lifecycle reachability from authoritative memberships/relations rather than separately storing a hold for every structural relationship. Semantic kinds need not become separate transactions or protection objects.

Preserve full collision refusal, exact selected ownership, legitimate equal-row reuse, new-holder retirement exclusion and safe ownership of partial producer effects. Extents/root collection must handle cross-contribution shared content and new references racing collection. Fusion increases rollback/retry scope and potentially live state; extent collection trades fine-grained reclamation for less metadata. The target must compare complete lifecycles, not only insert latency.

A unique exact prepared guard map is a valid target contract. Ordinary [expectation preparation](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/coordination.rs#L255) already constructs unique identities. Preserving first-entry behavior for ambiguous duplicate packets is not itself a functional requirement. Any normalized-map replacement must preserve native equality, incarnation checks and legitimate equal-successor joins, with its input-contract transition explicit.

## 2. Per-window recovery and durability machinery

<a id="NI-F02"></a>
**NI-F02 — Implemented recovery-granularity amplification.** Each successful immutable window captures expectations, hashes its exact SQL/bindings, durably records a pending envelope, applies payload/outcome/reference atomically, durably records terminal knowledge, captures current issuance, settles the reference in another transaction and deletes the local pending envelope. See [preparation](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L696), [owner-effect lowering](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L840), [execution](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L928), [settlement body](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L1036) and [reference release](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L1167).

For a fresh successful request, [pending preparation](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/recovery.rs#L560), [terminal observation](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/recovery.rs#L708) and [settlement](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/recovery.rs#L757) normally invoke two durable envelope writes and one removal. [Recipient write/remove](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/signal.rs#L114) performs four `sync_all` calls per write—temporary file, directory and two ancestors—and one on removal: nine calls in that normal path. Directory validation, serialization and replacement also occur. This is a source count of synchronous durability operations, not a claim about their elapsed-time share, actual storage-device flushes or dominance of recorded native apply.

**Target and alternatives — Proposed.** Compare producer/batch-owned durable journal and replayable progress against immediate settlement of every physical window. Grouped durable registration and terminal checkpoints could amortize host work and reference release while retaining bounded database transactions. The existing [history collector](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/history.rs#L605) commits effects, progress and an exact page result together and [replays the acknowledged page](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/history.rs#L397). Its [direct transaction delivery](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/history.rs#L686) is a useful operation-shaped precedent, not a transferable assumption that compilation is drained maintenance.

Preserve exact original requests and authority, metadata durable before first issue, terminal knowledge durable before releasing the last witness, classified retry, and actual retained recipients after Unknown outcomes. Removing references in the payload transaction alone can lose the last witness before host terminal knowledge survives a crash. Grouping without durable ownership is also insufficient. The tradeoff is longer-lived protected history and a more substantial journal operation, against fewer durability and settlement cycles.

This alternative changes the current immediate-success-settlement choice recorded by the storage owner. Keeping that choice limits improvement to reducing physical windows and optimizing their existing settlement path.

## 3. Generic incidence repeatedly invokes complete lifecycle cuts

<a id="NI-F03"></a>
**NI-F03 — Implemented lifecycle amplification.** [native_touch](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql#L167) invokes a complete attempt cut for an attempt target. [Validation and cut](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql#L311) check all64 stripe children through scalar locked reads and update all64.

Cleanup pages use `EffectOwner::Cleanup`. [Generic authorization](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L878) touches that owner and applies owner incidence; [cleanup incidence](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql#L172) includes the terminal origin attempt. Thus a later [cleanup page](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/cleanup.rs#L145) can repeat a complete attempt cut through its wrapper, even after ordinary production has been fenced.

**Target — Proposed.** Define a complete terminal-cleanup page operation that distinguishes permanent producer fencing from continuing collector and reference-incidence hazards. Carry the terminal fence through preserving operations and establish the exact changed-incidence exclusion needed by each page. Removing the cut without considering history deletion and context-free reference acquisition would weaken real guarantees.

Useful nonmatches constrain the remedy:

- [Freeze](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/frozen.rs#L49), [close](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control.rs#L725), semantic admission and [physical completion](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/completed.rs#L300) are actual eligibility transitions; their complete cuts have a real purpose.
- [Page incidence helpers](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/coordination.rs#L87), [pin release](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control.rs#L713) and [cleanup](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/cleanup.rs#L186) already deduplicate endpoint writes and bulk-increment predicates.
- [Retirement hold pages](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/retirement.rs#L790) already group validation and mutation, preserve lineage, and nominate children atomically with hold deletion. [Finalization](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/retirement.rs#L863) fetches incidence fields rather than full compiler/original bodies.

Changing the cut realization requires an alternative race argument. Keeping the current realization retains this64-stripe work; a fixed page size does not make it disappear.

## 4. Repeated preparation, representation and assurance work

<a id="NI-F04"></a>
**NI-F04 — Implemented internal work amplification.** Controlled immutable payloads undergo several preserving passes before issue:

- [Physical graph construction](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/adapter.rs#L200) clones canonical bytes and reparses graph JSON to recover an anchor available from the typed graph value.
- [Unit construction](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/immutable_window.rs#L14), [immutable planning](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control.rs#L380), [coalescing](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control.rs#L464) and emission repeat native-size validation.
- [plan_bytes](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/immutable_window.rs#L224) JSON-counts units and complete preparations; [payload hashing](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs#L583) materializes SQL/bindings JSON; [wire preflight](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/coordination.rs#L103) clones values into protobuf; [transaction retry](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/retry.rs#L203) clones SQL/bindings for each issue.

**Target — Proposed.** Prepare units once, carry established identity/size properties through preserving operations and produce the complete packet for hashing, sizing and issue. Pass the already-known typed anchor rather than reparsing it. Receiving validation, independent semantic admission, exact request hashing and actual wire/resident limits remain distinct obligations; the correction must not remove them indiscriminately.

The [128-row target](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/loader.rs#L1023) is an internal physical choice, not a functional promise. Raising it alone leaves expanded work and may increase conflicts, rollback cost and live state. Current summed unit accounting intentionally retains repeated overhead; it is not the exact size of the coalesced packet. Packing should follow complete resident/wire bounds and the useful operation. Parsed-query/encoded-request reuse needs qualification against the exact SDK; this inquiry establishes neither server parsing cost nor such API support.

Native-call counts also need attribution. [NativeCalls](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/native_calls.rs#L64) owns submission, recovery context, acknowledgement and joins. [Scoped preparation](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/admission/scoped.rs#L309) acquires each selected view before reuse lookup, and [reuse](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/admission/scoped.rs#L372) revalidates retained capabilities. However, [table streams](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler_provider.rs#L458) do not submit one NativeCall per delivered row; [selected streams](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler_provider.rs#L731) retain preparation and open transfer windows. [Content-token discovery](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/consumed_rows.rs#L2033) is once per nonempty bound port under a OnceCell. Many cheap memo completions are not evidence of many database round trips. Any grouped capability operation must preserve the store executor and joined descendant ownership, rather than return accepted work to a short-lived provider runtime.

## 5. Observation helpers fork sessions outside their explicit close ownership

<a id="NI-F05"></a>
**NI-F05 — Implemented session-lifecycle mismatch, exact-source checked.** Vendored [SurrealDB3.3.0](../../../../third_party/surrealdb/Cargo.toml#L12) implements [Surreal::clone](../../../../third_party/surrealdb/src/lib.rs#L406) by allocating a fresh session UUID and queueing a clone operation. The gRPC engine [attaches and replays the prior session log](../../../../third_party/surrealdb/src/engine/remote/grpc.rs#L840), including authentication/namespace selection, through its [lifecycle FIFO](../../../../third_party/surrealdb/src/engine/remote/grpc.rs#L1736).

[inspect_expected](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/held.rs#L49), [inspect_expected_window](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/held.rs#L499) and [inspect_hold_window](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/held.rs#L593) accept `&Surreal` and create `Arc::new(client.clone())`. These clone the SDK object, not an existing Arc. Their [private reader](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/reader.rs#L3227) defaults to [owns_session=false](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/reader.rs#L269); [reader close](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/reader.rs#L1367) explicitly detaches only owned sessions. SDK [Drop](../../../../third_party/surrealdb/src/lib.rs#L422) queues its session-drop event without the helper awaiting that terminal acknowledgement.

Production singleton callers include [staging readback](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/staging.rs#L102), [retained contribution acquisition](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L3696), [terminal candidate admission](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L3799) and [held-candidate relation scans](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L6747). Other current window-helper callers found in the inspected Rust sources are predominantly controls. This establishes extra session work and missing explicit helper-owned session finality, not permanent leakage or a measured contribution to the900-second control.

**Target — Proposed.** Borrow the actual Arc/client or existing reader/recovery owner for observation. Fork an independent session only when a consumer needs an independent lifetime, then register and acknowledge its closure. [PreparedQuery::stream](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/prepared.rs#L99) already deliberately preserves the original session to avoid this SDK behavior. No generic connection pool is implied. Independent completed readers that legitimately outlive their source must retain their own session authority.

## 6. Captured-row verification repeatedly searches owner holdings

<a id="NI-F06"></a>
**NI-F06 — Implemented repeated broad access under typed row intent.** [Captured-table admission](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/admission/candidate.rs#L213) sequentially calls [verify_captured_row](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/product_recovery.rs#L168) for every expected captured metadata row. Each call re-encodes the row, derives its exact expected physical identity, reconstructs the selected contributor set, then queries native_hold using owner_holds while filtering dereferenced object semantic type/key. Pagination asks for one match per request and orders by hold ID. Each match then receives protection and a separate singleton observation.

The decisive query is at [product_recovery.rs:262](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/product_recovery.rs#L262). The [owner_holds index](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql#L54) is on `(owner,object)`, not dereferenced semantic attributes. Thus row-wise verification can repeatedly examine unrelated holdings under the same selected contributors. This path is not established as active in the failed recursive cold compilation.

**Target — Proposed.** Verify a typed set of captured rows against its selected contributor closure, preserving all nominal candidates and conflicting-payload refusal, then batch physical observations and protection. Existing [membership declarations](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/installation.rs#L48), [relation/key selection](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/selection.rs#L1269) and [scalar-key lookup](../../../../crates/lctx-compiler-store/src/compiler.rs#L9895) provide relevant access shapes. A direct lookup of the expected content-addressed ID alone would lose detection of a conflicting payload at the same nominal identity. Owner qualification and complete candidate coverage are prerequisites, not optional speed tradeoffs.

The same set-observation opportunity occurs in [OriginalCompletion](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/originals.rs#L753): all layout IDs have been collected, but each layout receives separate layout, [artifact validation](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/originals.rs#L251) and ownership queries. Independent layout validity remains necessary; physical observations can be grouped. The later unique-blob pass correctly shares byte readback/CAS after all selected layout drainage and must not be described as per-layout blob hashing.

## Inspected breadth, counterexamples and limits

All17 production functions in [control/schema.surql](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/schema.surql) were inspected: touch, incidence-target projection, incidence, backup-clear, generic incidence-insert, contribution/completed incidence, immutable-install, attempt initialize/validate/cut/participate, admitted-check/touch/incidence/insert. Their important distinctions are:

- Backup-clear is one gate observation; attempt initialization creates64 children at allocation.
- Ordinary attempt participation validates one durable participant and advances one stripe; lifecycle cuts use the complete set.
- Generic incidence-insert had no ordinary Rust production caller found in the searched crate sources. The admitted counterpart is reached through guarded mutable creation. Ordinary immutable windows use the grouped installation function. Installed generic counterparts are not automatically current hot-path causes.
- Pin acquisition/release, exact expected-record/hold observations, frozen/live completion, product recovery/release, cleanup, retirement and history counterparts were inspected alongside the primary write path.

Other useful nonmatches:

- [Serving scope](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/scope.rs#L211) packages multiple scalar indexed statements per request and batches hydration. This retains per-root server work while avoiding per-object network crossings; it is not recursive-compilation evidence.
- [Lexical posting selection](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/lexical_stats.rs#L490) deliberately uses document-by-term point queries to reach both compound-index dimensions; the pinned planner's two independent IN lists otherwise scan broadly. Aggregation is shared. Replacing scalar maps without qualifying the actual plan can worsen access.
- [Alias topology](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/alias_topology.rs#L40) prepares forward/reverse indexes and [merged visited runs](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/alias_topology.rs#L154); it avoids repeated whole-history copying on long chains.
- [Embedding cache](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/cache.rs#L50) observes exact-key sets and [reconciles classified writes](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/cache.rs#L204), retrying missing winners rather than provider work. Its excluded embedding workload and independent contract do not support a blind finding against its batch/retry constants.

The immutable function retains several derived arrays and existing-record observations inside one atomic operation. Client window/request reservations do not establish a server evaluator/transaction working-set bound: [ingress admission](../../../../crates/lctx-native/src/reader/ingress.rs#L1) explicitly covers owned client buffers. Server allocation amplification, temporary storage and index timing remain unmeasured.

Managed service source configures [20-second query and10-second transaction limits](../../../../scripts/surrealdb_service.py#L790), plus a [128MiB gRPC ceiling](../../../../scripts/surrealdb_service.py#L51). These are individual-operation settings, not the900-second composed control; many successful transactions or retryable conflicts can consume its outer allowance. No setting change is recommended and no live service settings were inspected.

Preservation constraints across the six groups are exact typed identity and full collision checking, distinct equal-row/new-holder behavior, incarnation/tombstone correspondence, unchanged original requests through retry, atomic payload/outcome visibility, independent semantic and receiving assurance, and durable ownership of Unknown outcomes. Current fixed grains, first-match handling of malformed duplicates and generic protocol decomposition are replaceable mechanisms.

Representation/protection and recovery-granularity decisions interact: larger packets with the same expanded lifecycle metadata may only move the bottleneck; grouped recovery without durable ownership is unsafe. Narrow preparation reductions and session-owner correction can be assessed independently. A terminal-cut correction first needs its exact history/reference hazard contract. Consequence priority and prerequisite order are therefore distinct.

Potential rule changes belong to the principal review: root/extent protection changes the current exact hold/predicate realization under storage §6 and ADR-0145/0155/0178; grouped successful settlement changes the current immediate-settlement choice; reduced terminal cuts change the current attempt-cut realization. Keeping those rules narrows the respective remedies to packet fusion, local preparation/access improvements and fewer existing protocol invocations. Packet-local indexing, removal of internal conversion passes and changing the128-row target do not inherently change public semantic guarantees.

Searches covered production Rust in native/compiler-store and adjacent publisher/serving paths, relevant Python service configuration, and schema functions. Python/scripts were searched for matching native-control implementations; no corresponding implementation was found in that bounded scope. Cold verify_state, public definition/catalog breadth, derived-search scans, public manifest/admission breadth, output cardinality and exact library alternative coverage remain with the principal review's other inquiries. No absent API or unavailable engine capability is inferred from an empty search. All execution checks were **not_run**, as assigned.
