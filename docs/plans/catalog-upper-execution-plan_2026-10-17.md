## 2. Foundations and intended architecture

The source review's A1–A3 strengths remain valuable: model-owned facts and domains; exact immutable completed inputs; independent receiving; explicit Unknown/NotRequested; indexed native access; coalesced graph preparation; charged root windows; actual task/native joins; canonical callee-first SCCs and semantic progress worklists. Repeated scans and broad barriers are not part of the promised meaning merely because the current implementation has them.

The foundation assessment for this proposal inspected the actual native earned selector, model inventory/expected-domain inputs, indexed root positions, stage execution, analytic Correspondence/concept preparation and Summary/scheduling owners. It supports extending current owners instead of introducing a universal executor, proof registry, second canonical store, global rich cache or another thread pool.

Three products remain distinct:

1. **Protected acquisition:** exact observed content, complete promised coverage and current trust/lifecycle premises at a particular producer or receiving boundary.
2. **Consumer preparation:** compact properties, membership partitions, positions, indexes or topology derived from compatible protected inputs for actual borrowers.
3. **Semantic results:** owner-specific applicability, analyses, proofs, evidence and outcomes with fresh exact invocation/source identity.

Sharing the first two never grants another owner's semantic result, receiving admission or current provenance. A later full-logical demand may require additional acquisition if a compact preparation deliberately did not retain backing rows. An immutable value or equal digest is not a live owner.

No public CLI/wire change, fact relabelling, new analytic default or support-limit change is assumed. Internal preparation, sufficient-property, readiness and worker-integration contracts change. Where implementing them changes a persisted model/schema/definition identity, explicitly schedule the actual snapshot/owner/cutover work and regenerate project views from pinned inputs. Keep no old-format reader, rollback copy or compatibility-only code/data. Protected Unknowns and cited evidence are real current consumers and remain protected until their authenticated owner settles them.
