# Catalog completion: production contracts and preparation

**Production target contracts, 2026-10-14.** This production companion specifies the outcome, admission,
backing and preparation corrections needed for a credible pinned API/evidence Catalog. It develops
[implementation-friction F01–F08](../design_review/reviews/design_review_implementation-friction-target_2026-10-14.md#F01)
and preserves the delivered compiler, analysis and native foundations. Inspected authoring baseline:
`f330bf1d972ddab4962ffd7d9b1d4bd92b9d71b2`. The coordinator owns current implementation and evidence;
P01's delivered finality contract does not establish P02–P06 or assembled Catalog acceptance.

The [coordinator §6](catalog-completion-plan_2026-10-14.md#6-sole-disposition-and-current-checkpoint)
alone owns finding dispositions, decisions, execution state and receipts. Its decision table owns
the settled D01–D13 choices and revisit triggers, plus I01/I02 investigation outcomes. This document
owns their production reasoning and implementation packages; it is not another progress ledger.
The [acceptance companion](catalog-completion-acceptance-plan_2026-10-14.md) owns final functional
scope, the remaining genuine Summary/Catalog/cold/pilot exits, regeneration and precise handback.

## 1. Target, foundations and preservation

The resulting compiler retains required completed production when optional reuse preparation is
declined, distinguishes an operation refusal from ownership finality, admits nested allocation
before growth, and keeps charges with actual backing and borrowers. Exact immutable ingredients
serve compatible consumers without retaining every obsolete aggregate or repeatedly rebuilding
unchanged transitive preparation. Coherent multi-output handoff remains explicit. These changes
serve one operator with concurrent providers, release/corpus inputs, many shared vocabularies,
large selectors and overlapping dependency chains, including cancellation and spill.

The current system already provides much of the necessary foundation. Model-owned readiness and
ordered roles select exact completed contributors independently of arrival. Native union assembly
checks actual nominal conflict/count validity before replacing current selections. Scoped semantic
evidence is earned through real execution under complete premises; final freeze and independent
receiving/cold admission remain distinct. The native audit shares protected observations and typed
descriptors; canonical runs already support charged backing, independent cursors, spill and complete
union folds. Completion reports already distinguish local terminality, remote certainty, storage
outcomes and committed effects. P01–P06 improve their composition instead of replacing them with
a generic workflow, cache, proof registry or resource framework.

The focused foundation assessment uses core/template3.3, heuristics1.0 and code-intelligence1.5.
The material execution choices are lifetime and grain: prepare compatible stable inputs once,
retain only actual demand, and separate necessary exact output from avoidable reconstruction.
Honest refusal is required but cannot substitute for a feasible route. Structural controls can
establish removed repeated work or dead allowance; reservations are not RSS measurements and no
latency, throughput or capacity improvement is promised without measurement.

The following contracts constrain every package:

- Model-owned logical identities, ordered input roles, profile/model/configuration, requested and
  resolved boundaries, coverage, NotRequested/empty/Partial/failed/unavailable distinctions, exact
  completed selectors and selected vocabulary validity remain observable.
- Native source client/session/pin/generation/validity/resource-pool correspondence, positive
  incarnations, full descriptors and expected bytes, complete EOF/digests, child/parent holds,
  root fences, actual outcomes and final guarded reconciliation remain required. Serialized
  descriptors, receipts, equality and caches never grant another owner authority.
- Flat six-family semantic v4, native key/order/null/byte/conflict semantics and published witnesses
  remain the baseline. The full physical selector mapping output and eager cumulative visibility
  also remain until P05 qualifies and explicitly selects a complete alternative. Neither is
  changed merely because permission to investigate a change exists.
- Independent cold/import/receiving admission executes its own required checks. Same-operation
  ingredients cannot become portable validity, and cold equivalence is not established solely by
  replaying the producer's own normalizer.
- Canonical source capture, latest Ruff/Pyrefly/ty roles, required dependency imports and distinct
  release/corpus resolver contexts remain. Catalog compact owners retain their exact frame/domain
  premises; async compact reads await batches. Synchronous provider callbacks retain their separate
  bridge and accepted ingress/provider join ownership.
- BDD bounds, SCC schedules, per-seed traversal, ordered qualified witnesses, Summary candidate
  admission/PairOutcomes/proof evidence and analytic layer partial/exhaustion policies remain.
  This plan does not reopen D01–D13 or substitute reachability for their semantics.

## 2. Decisions, alternatives and package dependencies

The operator approved authoring these corrections and the review's RC01/RC02/RC05 directions on
2026-10-14. Those tooling/model-routing changes are coordinated outside this production companion.
RC03 permits persisted physical factorization and RC04 permits demand-only cumulative visibility
**conditionally after investigation**; neither alternative is selected. P05 supplies their decision
evidence. If selected, the coordinator records the outcome and schedules the affected architectural
owner/decision and complete consumer migration before dependent implementation. Authoring creates
no ADR and changes no rule. Bounded scratch, selector sharing, charged growth and outcome fixes
implement existing contracts and need no rule change merely to correct their composition.

| Package | Required working prerequisite | Delivered behavior and consumer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [P01](#p01) — outcome and finality | Existing Completion semantics and actual dispatcher ownership | Async calls and synchronous bridge retain operation diagnostics separately from actual task/owner finality; Workspace/artifact/CLI consumers compose both truthfully |
| [P02](#p02) — optional effect boundary | P01's usable result/finality distinction for submitted optional work | Pre-effect optional decline leaves required completed views usable; accepted effects retain explicit settlement and poisoning/Unknown behavior |
| [P03](#p03) — pre-admitted nested growth | Shared ResourceBudget/StateCharge and HeapSize contracts | Owned append/insert/mutation operations reserve before growth, with all affected model callers migrated |
| [P04](#p04) — native backing and selectors | Existing independent inventory admission; P03 where shared nested operations are used | Scratch ends with its work, retained payload stays charged, and capabilities share one exact-context immutable selector payload |
| [P05](#p05) — ingredients, demand and assurance | I01/I02 source inquiry can begin immediately; implementation consumes P03/P04 where its containers/factories change | Selected exact union/preparation route with explicit demand release and preserved root assurance; conditional physical/visibility alternatives fully migrated if selected |
| [P06](#p06) — narrow completion and integration | Working P01/P02 outcome/effect contracts and selected P05 preparation/visibility semantics | Private preparation leaves coherent commit exclusion where safe; all actual consumers use the delivered contracts before final acceptance |

P01 and P03 are useful early shared corrections. P04 is largely independent of P02; P05's bounded
investigations do not wait for unrelated implementation. P06 cannot move optional work out of the
gate before P02 settles its effect/failure boundary, or speculate about mutable aggregate visibility
before P05 settles that route. Agreement on an interface permits consumer design, not functional
completion. Shared edits in Workspace, compiler.rs and installed-view factories require coordinated
ownership even where logical dependencies are independent. No package boundary automatically
requires an assembled test campaign.

## 3. Production packages

<a id="p01"></a>
### P01 — preserve operation outcomes and complete ownership metadata

**Source obligation:** implementation-friction F01 and preservation of completion-integrated#F03.
NativeCalls currently turns a late error into the registered task's error when its acknowledgement
receiver is gone; NativeBridge has the corresponding adaptation. Workspace inserts their aggregate
errors into finality before merging native finality. The presence of a receiver must not determine
whether an acknowledged refusal is represented as unsuccessful drainage.

Define a small shared result carrier or paired reporting operations at the dispatcher boundary.
Accepted work produces its operation result and its actual ownership report. Dispatchers retain
late operation diagnostics when delivery disappears, join all submitted work, and preserve panic,
failed join, local Outstanding and genuine remote Unknown. A joined outer task does not establish
that every owner nested inside its operation was settled. Unwrap contextual/SharedCause wrappers
only through the owning completion semantics; retain nested Completion storage outcomes, committed
effects, failures and worst certainty rather than flattening them to a benign Resource/Schema error.
Successful metadata also must not disappear through conversion to `Result<(), _>`.

Workspace composes actual dispatcher/provider/store/cache ownership into finality and settles its
existing immutable signal before appending sticky acknowledged operation diagnostics to the caller.
The attempt can remain failed/poisoned while actual cleanup is confirmed. Conversely, an operation
refusal with unknown cleanup remains protected. Preserve cancellation registration, task/bridge
runtime ownership and retryable drainage when a drain future itself is dropped; no owner becomes
unregistered because its caller stops receiving. Repeated drain keeps genuine finality failures
visible without counting one acknowledged refusal as a fresh failed join on every call.

**Exact consumer scope.** Migrate NativeCalls, NativeBridge, WorkspaceExecutionOwner and all their
drain/result consumers, including synchronous provider access, write/close dispatch, artifact shared
execution ownership, restore/CLI signal composition and the affected fixture-facing reporting path.
Inspect callers of aggregate and finality-only APIs together; do not leave a wrapper silently
reintroducing the old conflation. Keep fixture classification strict and receipt meanings intact.

**Functional evidence.** Pair live and dropped acknowledgement receivers with an acknowledged
refusal and actual successful drainage: required production remains failed, diagnostics survive,
and finality is clean. Pair the same primary with nested local Outstanding, remote Unknown,
storage orphan/removal and committed-effect metadata; no field may be lost. Include panic/failed
thread join, cancellation during drainage followed by retry, and a borrowed batch/charge surviving
until accepted task terminality. Existing cancelled-caller tests need the composed finality assertion,
not weaker error matching. Pure dispatcher controls precede the affected actual fixture consumer.

<a id="p02"></a>
### P02 — decline optional capture before mandatory effects

**Source obligation:** implementation-friction F02. `Workspace::retain_product` advertises a local
Resource/Limit decline, but reaches mandatory dispatcher failure/cancellation and a poison-on-error
mutation lease first. Merely finding a wrapped Resource error cannot undo those effects or prove
that the retained boundary left no obligation.

Separate required producer completion from optional reusable-product capture. The capture owner
receives the exact completed request/contribution/context and either declines during effect-free,
fully owned preparation or delivers preparation ready for explicitly admitted native effects.
Decline is an explicit optional outcome, distinct from a successful empty product and from failed
required production. It leaves current required views usable and releases only its own scratch after
all accepted preparation work has settled. Mandatory dispatch must not poison the attempt merely
for that pre-effect decline. Keep actual preparation/cancellation ownership even when caller delivery
ends; a nonpoisoning flag without an effect/settlement contract is insufficient.

Preparatory reads or protection acquisition can themselves introduce owned effects. Identify them
explicitly rather than calling the existing retention routine pure: borrow an already protected
source where valid, or settle newly acquired preparation ownership conclusively before reporting a
decline. The pre-effect branch cannot conceal an unsettled pin, reader, worker or native request.

Identify the first native effect and the obligations of every subsequent failure. Once effects
begin, retain the existing fatal/protected behavior for uncertain or unsettled work. A separately
owned optional effect may permit a decline only if it conclusively settles its effects and lifetime;
that is an alternative requiring concrete source/functional evidence, not an error-label exception.
Replay of an existing exact retained product still checks its original request/contribution/retention
and boundary under actual protection. No incomplete product is silently overwritten or relabelled
absent. P01 carries nested metadata and late results; P02 decides what the operation means.

**Exact consumer scope.** Migrate Workspace product-request handling, compiler-store retention
admission, request gates, native retained boundary construction/replay and the product-cache/attached
candidate consumers affected by the chosen preparation/effect split. Required singleton completion,
selected cumulative assembly, native bind and current/frozen view handoff keep their mandatory
failure contract. Do not make all mutations optional to serve this one consumer.

**Functional evidence.** Complete required production, refuse an optional local reservation before
effects, and demonstrate that current views remain usable, publication prerequisites remain valid
and drainage is clean. Exercise a pre-effect cancellation with live prepared backing, a retained
replay, and post-effect failure/Unknown that still prevents publication and protects its obligations.
An independently specified successful empty output must remain distinguishable from declined
capture. Use the real Workspace→dispatcher→store route; a translation-only unit test is insufficient.

### P03 — move nested growth admission into the charged owner

**Source obligation:** implementation-friction F03. ChargedMap::update mutates first and charges
the final heap delta afterward. Its unrestricted callback can reallocate a nested vector/set or
create temporary backing before the shared pool decides whether it fits.

Provide capacity-aware owned append/set-insert operations, or an explicit pre-admitted mutation
allowance where the mutation has a declared footprint. The charged owner reserves the required new
capacity/entry and value heap before the growth operation; a refusal leaves prior state and charge
coherent. Preserve duplicate insert, replacement without growth, removal and shrinking without
requiring a full replacement allocation. A raw arbitrary mutation callback cannot retain the current
reserve-before-growth promise; remove it from growth paths rather than ask every caller to duplicate
Vec/B-tree accounting. Do not clone full values for rollback, which creates another allocation and
unnecessary live state. Existing StateCharge and HeapSize remain the accounting foundation.

**Exact consumer scope.** Find every production ChargedMap::update call and classify its actual
mutation: vector append, set insertion, scalar/no-growth update or a separately owned operation.
Migrate all allocating callers, including normalized entity/event/relation/callable/overload
preparation, selection, composition, read channels, evaluation and Summary progress/consequence
indexes. Representative sequence-members and Summary branch-set controls reveal the problem but
do not define the migration boundary. Inspect ChargedVec/ChargedSet and removed/replaced-value
transfers for consistent caller/backing ownership when adapting the shared API. Preserve codebooks,
domain row meaning, ordering, exact identity and semantic work limits.

**Functional evidence.** A refusing pool plus a growth/mutation witness proves refusal precedes
allocation and state change; the old value and reservation remain coherent. Cover vector capacity
expansion, a large nested heap, duplicate set insertion, no-growth update, removal and shrinking.
Then run focused normalization and Summary controls that consume the migrated APIs, retaining
PairOutcomes, proof/support identity and work/exhaustion meanings. A tiny budget is a revealing test
input, not a new production cap. Pure model verification needs no database or Python environment.

### P04 — bind native charges and selectors to their actual backing

**Source obligations:** implementation-friction F04/F05; preserve completion-integrated#F06/F07.
Boundary construction transfers a builder charge into NativeCompletedState after local builder
containers disappear; actual binding audit accumulates row decode allowance for all prior rows.
Retained view factories also clone an inventory selector and store both a completion and a second
deep SelectedViewDescriptor. Related frozen/lifecycle/published factories retain decode-style
allowances. Correct the responsibilities, not the pool size or an unexplained multiplier.

Use the native factory boundary to distinguish current decode/validation/serialization scratch,
new owned typed/native backing, and borrowed immutable backing whose original owner is retained.
Admit each before allocation. Keep temporary work through its last real borrower, then release it;
transfer only retained backing allowance into returned capabilities. For complete binding audits,
retain the necessary typed inventory and one current row's scratch rather than all past decode
allowances. Include container capacity, map nodes, Arc headers, pointer/sort workspace and typed
heaps where they actually exist. Values used to calculate allowance must not allocate an unadmitted
serialization first. Preserve failure/drainage ownership of in-flight native work and prepared spill.

Independent receiving inventory inspection remains. Scope builder sets, references and copied
contributors until their comparisons/effects finish, then release them, or consume independently
checked typed results without keeping a redundant complete builder copy. Keep all-view equality,
binding-selected semantics, physical-copy collision checks, complete child envelopes, pointer order,
parent/context/descriptor/incarnation/holds, membership EOF/digests and final CAS. Reaching EOF alone
does not release an expected row or run still borrowed by a pending effect.

Retained installed capabilities expose a borrowed selector or one shared immutable selector payload.
Its identity includes the actual capability/inventory and exact physical mapping context: the same
logical suffix can legitimately select different physical copies/incarnations under different roots.
Keep expected-envelope bytes and actual pins as distinct assurance/protection. Resolve mappings from
the retained inventory when it is the active source instead of populating another deep registry;
preserve mutable live-selection ownership and internal earned selectors that have no retained
compound boundary. In-memory sharing does not require a persisted codec or semantic-v4 migration.

**Exact consumer scope.** Cover both boundary return paths, actual frozen binding audit,
InstalledViewInventory, held frozen completion, lifecycle physical completion, published completion
and admission, all published/frozen/physical/recovery retained-view factories, the internal earned
view factory, and compiler-store physical/published inventory registration/mapping lookup. Include
direct consumers in sparse/selected native access, candidate attachment, restore/import, transport,
backup/publication and serving where these factories are borrowed. Inspect logical-template and
audit-cache lifetimes when their backing participates in this route; do not clear live caches merely
because a pin was closed. Any additional material route discovered has an owning package/closure
obligation before this package claims complete consumer migration.

**Functional evidence.** Hold a returned completed owner alive and show builder/row scratch release
while retained typed backing remains admitted. Exercise cross-window selector lookup, large mapping
clone/serialization refusal before allocation, one selector payload through retained capability
variants, last borrower/receiver drop and cancellation with accepted native work still running.
Pair release cases with late malformed/missing child, changed parent/incarnation, missing hold,
different physical realization, changed mapping/pool and independent cold admission refusals. Retain
literal v4 roots and expected byte/null distinctions. Accounted reservation observations can support
these lifetime claims; they must not be reported as measured RSS or a dominant historical cause.

### P05 — settle useful demand and direct assurance before selecting aggregate changes

**Source obligations:** implementation-friction F06/F07. The coordinator owns I01 and I02 outcomes;
the investigations here deliver their bounded technical basis. They may use source traces and
small revealing controls; no full Catalog, new database, benchmark campaign, semantic identity
change or cache limit is required merely to decide.

**I01: distinguish retained useful demand from repeated union work.** Trace the actual consumers
of membership_preparations/PreparedCandidates across exact changed contributor sets: current eager
assembly, earlier frozen input ports, running and remaining consumers, inherited/retained products,
count and subsequent scans. Identify which successful registry entries still have a prospective
consumer and which merely keep backing after useful demand ends. Borrowers retain their original
charges independently of registry membership. A latest-only eviction rule would invalidate genuine
earlier demand; an arbitrary cache size cannot answer this ownership question.

Separately trace the physical read/decode/sort/merge and conflict/count work for changing sets.
Exact-set reuse avoids replay of an identical set but does not remove overlapping prefix work;
registry release fixes retained backing, not repeated scans/sorts. Compare contributor-local ordered
fragments with bounded merge, a checked incremental current union, and the complete existing eager
route. Preserve actual nominal equal-content coalescing, unequal-content refusal, full EOF/count and
unchanged previous/current/frozen views on failure. Fragment hashes cannot be concatenated into
the v4 union, and registered metadata cannot replace actual selected-union validity.

Prefer the smallest qualified correction that keeps eager visibility: prepare compatible immutable
ingredients once and retire registry ownership by actual running/remaining demand. A demanded
aggregate may still warrant materialized backing for repeated reads. Establish that use before
retaining every prefix. If demand-only assembly is materially simpler for complete consumers,
RC04 permits its selection after this investigation, not before. Specify the exact changed
selection/visibility operation, all consumers, failure/cancellation behavior and publication-prefix
semantics; qualify the whole alternative before replacing eager handoff. The coordinator records
that choice and its architectural route. No transitional metadata-only success seam is acceptable.

**I02: distinguish necessary flat output from repeated assurance.** Trace selected mapping discovery
and installed audit across chains, reconvergent diamonds and multiple independent physical suffix
realizations. Native observations and typed descriptors already share some work. Identify repeated
local descriptor clones, dependency traversal/validation and owned closure maps separately from
the current codec's required full ordered selector arrays and their emission/hashing. Do not infer
a database query for every repeated element or call the whole exact output redundant.

Compare operation-local shared direct dependency/assurance ingredients under actual protected source,
context, exact physical correspondence and resource pool with the existing complete flat route.
The model's RecoveryTraversal or owned successor continues to define dependency identity, relation,
cardinality and completeness; native observation reuse does not create a second semantic interpreter.
Each root earns its mapping consistency, full membership content and final-state obligations.
Compatible borrowed ingredients can replace repeated owned recovery closure reconstruction without
turning one root's success into another root's semantic admission.

If necessary flat arrays remain materially burdensome, compare a complete persisted physical
factorization: fragment/edge identity, root-specific choices, copies/incarnations, current lookup,
expected-envelope validation, missing/extra/conflicting fragment refusal, full child inventory,
final publication and independent cold/recovery/backup. RC03 permits a physical codec/envelope
change after this evidence; it does not select a semantic Merkle/DAG identity. Equal logical suffixes
with different physical copies stay separate. Keep flat semantic v4 and canonical family folding.
Until all affected consumers of a chosen physical alternative are qualified, retain the complete
flat implementation; do not introduce an incomplete runtime reader or dual compatibility authority.

**Selected implementation and exact consumer scope.** After each investigation settles its bounded
choice, migrate compiler-store membership preparation/count/row scans, selection/dependency mapping,
native audit/direct ingredients and their current/frozen/retained consumers together. Demand release
includes running/remaining exact input selections, cache-hit and optional paths, last borrower,
cancellation and final drain. If P05 chooses only in-memory ingredients, it still closes both repeated
work and unsupported retention obligations identified by I01/I02; it cannot claim persisted arrays
were eliminated. If it chooses RC03/RC04, include every affected artifact/publication, sparse/read,
candidate/attachment, transport, backup/restore/import and cold consumer before the cutover is complete.

**Functional evidence.** Multiple shared-relation producers with disjoint, equal-overlap and conflicting
nominal content retain exact demanded rows/counts and independently specified old frozen views.
Show ingredient preparation and no-longer-demanded registry release through structural witnesses;
borrowed old runs remain valid until their consumers end. Chain/diamond controls retain literal flat
roots/output and distinct physical suffix choices, including late corruption in an unbound dependency.
Pair memory/mixed/spill success with local refusal, cancellation and complete drainage. New physical
or visibility choices require independent exact known answers and their full affected round trips;
one representation agreeing with its own decoder is insufficient. Measurement is needed only for
a quantitative benefit claim, not to hide known repeated work behind a passing fixture.

**Exact consumer scope.** Migrate ProducerOutput completion, selected binding/current-set composition,

## 4. Delivery, verification and remaining boundaries

During implementation, compile touched crates and run focused revealing controls for each delivered
contract with normal parallelism and explicit selection. Pure charged/model/finite controls need no
database. Native controls use existing fixture/service/run/storage ownership and exact permitted
targets; ordinary verification never creates a scratch fallback or synchronizes implicitly. Preview
the actual selection with `just verify --print --select FAMILY[:BOUNDARY]`; use an explicit affected
filter when running it. A bare fixture-backed command remains available through `just fixture --`.
No historical receipt or mock provider is a current-source functional pass.

P01–P06 deliver working consumers and remove replaced interpretation, mutation, duplicated selector
or obsolete preparation paths. A package is not complete at an agreed interface or helper unit test.
Structural identities/definitions change deliberately when meaning or physical codec changes; the
selected decision, affected executable ownership and current data regeneration travel together.
Retain no compatibility-only runtime reader or historical data prerequisite. Quiesce project readers
before replacing generated state, and release only authenticated owned resources after actual cleanup.
Unknown, cited evidence, shared/operator targets, warm caches and unrelated concurrent work remain
protected.

Independent review should inspect the outcome/admission changes and any selected P05 physical or
visibility alternative at their real consumer boundary. Scope-end affected controls and applicable
leaves run once, with exact commands/outcomes/revisions recorded only in coordinator §6. The
[acceptance companion](catalog-completion-acceptance-plan_2026-10-14.md) schedules scoped composed
acceptance for these shared contracts, final-source Summary/linked Catalog/current cold controls,
exclusive mutation controls, pinned FastMCP and regeneration/handback. Do not run those enclosing
exits after each slice or claim they were established by compile/Clippy, a body pass with failed
cleanup, corrected source or a successful small case.

The next production design decisions are I01's demanded aggregate preparation/retention route and
I02's shared assurance route, with physical factorization or demand-only visibility selected only
if their complete alternatives justify the change. P01/P03 can establish their shared contract
corrections while those investigations proceed. Their current readiness, outcomes and any broader
finding obligations stay solely with the coordinator.
