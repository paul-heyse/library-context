## 1. Purpose, boundaries and reading route

Make the complete pinned API/evidence Catalog and its supported Behavioral enrichment efficient
by removing avoidable repeated work, shared conflict addresses and unowned resource/recovery
admission. Preserve the semantic and operational protections that make their answers trustworthy.
The target includes captured providers, native effects, completion, independent receiving,
publication/query preparation, explicit Behavioral analyses and the development loop.

This plan does not supersede or change any existing plan. The stopped Catalog-completion track
retains its dispositions, interrupted evidence and V01–V05 exits. Older tracks remain paused;
their remaining work is not imported here. Once this scope is implemented and accepted, the
operator can authorize reconciliation of those plans before their resumption. Overlapping source
may improve their behavior, but cannot silently close their findings or acceptance.

Embedding inference, MCP-submitted runtime queries, operator activation, broad environment
qualification and a new real-library qualification campaign are outside this track. Native
code-intelligence generation and query preparation remain in scope. Behavioral improvements
are required for this track's explicit Behavioral support, not prerequisites for ordinary Catalog.

## 3. Combined target, contracts and alternatives

For full pinned releases, many fact families, overlapping dependencies, high-degree graphs,
concurrent producers and interrupted operations, ordinary work should follow the smallest
semantically sufficient input and actual consumer lifetime. Keep normal available parallelism.
Resource protection must admit a credible route, rather than hide amplification behind refusal,
thread limits or enlarged timeouts.

The shared structures are refinements of existing owners:

- **Native bounded effect lowering:** distinguish shared parent checks, exact object guards,
  collision comparison and grouped insertion. One window remains atomic; transport batching alone
  does not establish database work batching.
- **Admission and recovery:** admit actual participants/effects, establish a complete close/freeze
  cut, and govern new recovery issuance separately from awaiting an already issued terminal.
- **Charged immutable preparation:** share acquisition and correspondence under exact premises,
  while consumers retain their own root semantics, checks, errors, proofs and outcomes.
- **Ingress ownership:** compose framing, decode workspace, queued batches, delivered values,
  cancellation and physical finality. Session/pool ownership is not implied by an Arc.
- **Development execution:** authenticated owners and actual persisted requests govern cancellation;
  selected run discovery and evidence preparation follow their consumers.

No universal lifecycle enum, scheduler, retry framework, proof cache, permanent run database,
new hook or cost-accounting system is part of the target. A new mechanism needs a concrete
capability and a simpler total operation, including its construction, retirement and recovery.

### Preservation and change scenarios

Retain exact full-value conflict detection, equal-row coalescing, complete absence checks,
provider/profile/model/input/vocabulary context, root-specific physical copies/incarnations,
original bytes, independent receiving and unselected publication. Preserve NotRequested,
partial/exhausted/ambiguous outcomes and separate operation/host/native/storage certainty.
Shared preparation is never portable validity.

A new fact family must use the model declaration and relevant provider behavior without
multiplying unrelated preparation per root. Shared signatures and reconvergent graphs must
reuse immutable work without merging proofs or root budgets. A mechanism substitution must
preserve the selected universe, multiplicity, ordering, fidelity and failure semantics. Wide
streams, close/freeze races, concurrent backup/retirement and cancelled owners must have truthful
resource and terminal boundaries.

### Complete alternatives

The corrected shared-native route is the immediate baseline, not a rule that all computation
belongs in SurrealDB. Compare native set operations and selective joins with bounded Rust/DataFusion
preparation at the operation that can benefit. Local spillable compiler preparation followed by
independent native ingestion remains an eligible larger alternative for CC00/CC03 and CN00 if
the complete ownership, reuse, originals, receiving and recovery route is simpler.

Factored selectors, demand assembly and query-shaped postings are explicit investigations,
not predetermined migrations. Database-hosted WASM, another store or files/indexes need a
complete consumer/lifecycle advantage before selection. Library availability alone does not
schedule adoption. SurrealDB reference constraints do not replace the whole admission protocol;
table-wide BM25 does not equal selected-corpus scoring; declared Arrow encoding is unavailable
at the reviewed server. Events/live/changefeeds/timers, buckets, generic DB MCP, Cloud and graph
front ends have no newly established consumer. These conclusions can reopen on new evidence.
