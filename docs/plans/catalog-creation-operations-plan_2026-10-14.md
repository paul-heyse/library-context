# Catalog creation: lifecycle and development operations

**Proposed changed seams / inherited implemented foundations, 2026-10-14.** Companion to the
[coordinator](catalog-creation-plan_2026-10-14.md), whose §6 alone owns dispositions and receipts.
Preserves prior OP01–OP03/LO/LD obligations and C01–C11 decisions. Existing diagnostics, schema8,
beta integration and checked disposal are foundations, not projects to restart. Adapt only the seams
changed by the new compiler/analysis/native contracts and verify their affected behavior.

## 1. Lifecycle meaning and actual owners

Acquisition inputs, attempts, contributions, exact views, admitted artifacts, publications, selections,
pins, reusable products, backups, native obligations and host attachments have distinct authority,
mutability, visibility, eligibility, retention consumer, liveness, uncertainty and recovery needs.
Execution, semantic eligibility and retention are independent. No universal lifecycle enum/registry,
new scheduler or second release authority follows. Actual native certainty is not host process status;
successful test body, capture, local cleanup, committed effect and disposal remain separate axes.

Use [model completion](../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/completion.rs),
[native effect diagnostics](../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/authorization.rs),
[signal writer](../../crates/lctx-native/src/signal.rs),
[fixture observer](../../scripts/surrealdb_fixture.py),
[service owner](../../scripts/surrealdb_service.py) and
[development worker](../../tools/lctx-surreal-dev/src/main.rs).
ADR-0165/0167 retain explicit containment/quiescence/disposal, while the new target changes neither
operator adoption nor another paused campaign's authority.

## 2. CO01 — diagnostics and generated-definition integration

### Diagnostic producer/consumer seams

Keep stable bounded model-owned class/code/stage, secondary causes, optional known request/attempt/
generation/era identities and safe owner-specific inspection/recovery routes. Preserve opaque
pre-admission/third-party errors, explicit truncation and absent identities. Human text is not a
machine protocol. Codes remain append-only; diagnostic information grants no effect/disposal authority.

New readiness, scoped-validation, search-preparation and scratch owners must migrate their actual
signal/report consumers alongside implementation. Identify contribution, internal-selection,
retained-boundary or pure failure without fabricating a native attempt. Preserve original typed errors
and local/remote/storage/committed-effect axes. No historical receipt translator, SQL/credential leakage,
new event collector or universal validity register is needed.

Compose existing target/service generation, host run/attachment, native effect, exact boundary/pin/
publication, backup/retirement and evidence inspection when those identities exist. Show actual owner,
source/date, uncertainty and valid next route. Closed maintenance reports unobserved native state and
its maintenance diagnostic route; inspection must not reopen admission/query a closed installation.
Missing receipt, dead worker, logical DROP or empty host borrowers is not remote drainage.

On-demand EXPLAIN/index inspection and coarse phases remain useful. EXPLAIN ANALYZE executes and
needs an authorized workload owner. Plan JSON is diagnostic, not a stable public wire contract.
Keep source/recipe/binary/running generation, SDK/RPC patches, beta capabilities/version, schema/
validity/executable epochs and publication identity distinct. Borrowed executable observation uses
the original authenticated executable owner; campaign directory/recipe alone identifies no binary.

**Acceptance:** affected real writer→host/report round trip, opaque/absent identity and truncation,
failed command with committed effects, Unknown and cleanup failure; missing target/permission refusal
stays read-only and credential-safe. Unchanged passed cases do not rerun solely due plan replacement.

### Sole generated schema and beta tooling

Retain exact **SurrealKit1.0.0-beta.1** as development tooling, not the product SDK. Product SDK/RPC
remain the patched3.3.0 family; beta macro pin/vendor manifest patch and required HTTP/gRPC/rustls
features remain explicit in [provenance](../../third_party/surrealkit/README.lctx.md). No product CLI
dependency edge, unused defaults/local engine/JWKS or beta upgrade is implied. Move versions only
for an actual need, with narrow lock change and affected evidence.

Direct Sync uses separately configured owned Any sessions under the host binding. Clones are distinct
sessions; invalidation clears auth, not transaction rollback. Beta's public tester creates private
clients without repository injection: contain it in a wholly owned remote service with actual auth
and server-side capability ceilings. It cannot claim product patched progressive/cancellation finality.

The worker binds exact endpoint/namespace/database/generation/purpose/credential-reference/effect.
Campaign and installer descriptors agree before effects; actual validation-marker readback verifies
schema/generation. Current managed local gRPC and fresh `lifecycle_<32-lowercase-hex>` database in
`library_context` remain supported. Remote/TLS/API-request support is demand-triggered, not unfinished
default adoption. Sync/declarative tests use fail_fast=false, ordinary suite parallelism, keep_db=true
and JSON reports. Watch/seed/rollout/typegen/embedded engine/selectable fail-fast remain unexposed.

Native/model definitions remain sole authority. The generator emits ordered responsibility modules
for control/canonical/search/compiler/completion, with directory/content tracking and embedded bytes.
Ordinary Sync writes no checkout; no native build dependency duplicates the host closure. Named Sync
and default tester projection consume full ordered requested-module closure. Refuse unsupported
selectors rather than ignore them. Render lifecycle variables before beta hashing. Compare installed
required definitions with native authority, normalizing only application modifiers and preserving
function bodies, permissions, indexes, nested SQL and lifecycle names. Explicit beta bookkeeping/
fixtures do not certify unrelated objects. Syntax parsing is not installed permission/function evidence.

When selected target changes actually alter schema/definitions, version deliberately, regenerate
these same assets and migrate parser/projection/installed-definition/index inspection in that package.
Otherwise reuse schema8 outcomes without pretending to perform another migration. Generated assets
exclude native's shared mutable function templates; immutable executable epochs remain publication-owned.
An explicit narrow native application of the same asset on the same owned target is preferable to
silently dropping an unsupported transformation. No introspection-based second schema authority.

## 3. Whole-target containment and meaningful validation

Ordinary native controls borrow installed logical validation through existing owners. Privileged
schema/permission/destructive beta controls use an explicitly owned disposable whole-service campaign.
Register exact purpose/generation/endpoint/datastore/credentials/consumer before launch. Private beta
root clients never attach to shared/operator targets; old paused campaigns are not spare environments.

Parent ownership survives setup failure/no report/unexpected suite databases. Authenticate and drain
workers/submitted native owners before exact daemon shutdown and storage/credential/installer release.
Journal bounded disposal-only logical actions before launch and reconcile retries. Failed logical drain
plus confirmed whole-executor discard can settle that target's disposal while original failed/Unknown
records remain unchanged. Unknown local/native executor drainage retains the obligation. Cancellation,
local attachment release and generic sweeps are not native disposal.

ADR-0167 allows narrow ordinary dead-maintenance transfer only under fresh exact participant/executor
authentication and journaled storage/coordination exclusion. Live/foreign/malformed/specialized recovery,
restore/reconciliation/SOURCE_JOIN markers remain blocking. Historical marker-child ancestry is not
required when all current supported participants are authenticated/quiescent. Future maintenance enrolls
managed children before spawn; explicit older run references add current-quiescence obligations without
inventing target/ancestry. Preserve exact target/configuration/run bindings and original outcomes.

Token substitution journals original placeholders before effects and restores through exact private
path/campaign/generation/run/storage checks, atomically after authenticated worker drainage. Do not adopt
an old project without its journal; restoration precedes credential-removal completion.

**Affected acceptance:** required native/root/record/token authentication, generated Sync/definition
readback and declarative reports; setup failure before capture; committed marker before cancelled
SLEEP; token-SIGTERM restoration; private-session versus worker cleanup; checked whole-target discard.
Keep failures before capture/after commit/during restoration/disposal distinct. Pure transforms,
owned schema controls and actual product admission/publication establish different claims. No broad
tool family, container, telemetry platform, historical capture or operator activation is required.

## 4. Retained C01–C11 platform choices

package references updated to this track. They are not a list of libraries to adopt or fresh runtime
qualification. New D02/D03/D04/D05 and the combined workload may reopen a named premise; record that
decision at coordinator §6 and integrate any selected migration/acceptance before dependent delivery.

server identified in the [versioned research](../design_review/evidence/2026-10-12_surrealdb-lifecycle-target/library-contracts.md).
Those Proposed/Interface-checked alternatives are not new version qualification. New documentation
questions use Context7 first, then exact pinned primary source. A needed probe uses an explicitly

### Source basis and limits of the decisions

The decisive evidence was read directly, not inferred from feature names or an empty search:

- **Retention/events:** pinned `core/src/doc/field.rs:877–944` writes reference keys and optional inline state; `doc/purge.rs:455–542` scans backlinks and applies REJECT/CASCADE. `doc/event.rs:146–172,489–526` persists asynchronous enqueue and removes exhausted/final-error events. Application [retirement](../../crates/lctx-native/src/control/retirement.rs) retains exact incarnation nomination and recoverable effects. Native reference concurrency remains unqualified; that is not a demonstrated engine defect and is not a premise of the keep-current decision.
- **Sweeps:** [Storage.retire](../../scripts/storage_lifecycle.py), lines 1085 onward, reacquires exclusive storage admission, owner and metadata guards before current disposition and its journaled effect. `sweep`, lines 1198 onward, invokes that owner per candidate. Its operation-level reference snapshot is not claimed to prove every concurrent reference edit safe. Timer adoption cannot improve that semantic boundary; it would need the same current rechecks and independent interruption controls.
- **Graphs/retrieval:** pinned `server/src/ntw/gql.rs:1–85` is an active session/capability/timeout HTTP route; `idx/src/inline_cache/mod.rs:1–37` documents snapshot equivalence, spill and a cap-change invalidation race. [SelectedPayloads](../../crates/lctx-native/src/selection.rs) already owns exact physical selection. [Search](../../crates/lctx-serving/src/search.rs), lines 291–345 and 384–586, retains eligible occurrences, exact-view scoring and exact vector identity. [Lexical statistics](../../crates/lctx-native/src/lexical_stats.rs) and [ranking](../../crates/lctx-model/src/domain/serving/ranking.rs), especially lines 435–475, establish why table-wide statistics or one-level RRF is not an equivalent replacement. Approximation is a semantic choice, not merely an index toggle.
- **APIs/modules/buckets:** pinned `core/src/api/invocation.rs:117–185` changes permission enforcement; `server/src/ntw/api.rs:129–145` restricts HTTP body representations. `core/src/legacy/expr/statements/define/module.rs:100–137` logs failed module warming while retaining the definition. `remove/bucket.rs:43–56` removes only metadata; `core/src/kvs/export.rs:147–150` emits bucket definitions. These concrete additional obligations support the negative substitution decisions without pretending their complete runtime behavior was tested.
- **Diagnostics/MCP/storage alternatives:** pinned `server/src/cli/validate.rs:30–91` parses without executing definitions; `cli/mcp.rs:20–141` creates a datastore and owner session. `ntw/mcp.rs` provides a capability-gated HTTP general database surface; `telemetry/mod.rs` supplies actual community OTel/log configuration. `core/src/exec/operators/explain.rs:49–54,85–88` distinguishes plain EXPLAIN, which formats a plan without execution, from EXPLAIN ANALYZE, which executes it. Analyze must therefore be an explicitly authorized bounded read workload under its actual owner, not an arbitrary SQL side effect hidden inside inspection. [Selected backup](../../crates/lctx-publisher/src/backup.rs), lines 13–92 and 95–138, retains its pin, one transaction-bound export, explicit cancellation/invalidation and distinct cold-recovery metadata. The [storage/publication owner](../design/sections/storage-and-publication.md) defines the complete shared catalog route compared in C11.

`core/`, `idx/` and `server/` above resolve beneath the exact retained upstream checkout named in the [library-contract evidence](../design_review/evidence/2026-10-12_surrealdb-lifecycle-target/library-contracts.md#source-identities). Source was inspected without opening its installation or executing its server. No source inspection here is a new version qualification. Current diagrams/claims outside these selected consumers, arbitrary engine backends and future deployment needs remain unexamined.

### Delivery consequences

C09 is a positive adoption through already required CO01, not an optional production rollout or a separate status ledger. The other rows select the current complete route or a concrete demand-triggered optional integration; none requires an optional new production mechanism in the retained lifecycle foundation. This conclusion does not waive lifecycle#F01–F05, the generated-beta integration, actual diagnostics or their runtime acceptance. A supported future adoption must add its implementation/consumer migration and acceptance package before execution; the current decision rows are not perpetual prohibitions.

### Investigation results must be usable

A decision cannot read merely “investigate later.” State the question, evidence examined, which supported outcome follows, and what implementation is then required. For C01/C08 and other correctness-sensitive substitutions, uncertainty means retaining the established protected route while the decision package gathers evidence. No unsupported native primitive is allowed into a production prerequisite. If evidence disproves the benefit or shows no current consumer, record that result and a concrete future trigger instead of inventing infrastructure to use the feature.

A retained-witness decision belongs to CC00; this document does not create another witness owner. Beta feature/API correctness belongs to CO01. Common native definition/session/ownership premises are settled once and linked, not independently redefined for each capability.

## 5. Current-state regeneration and older-plan reconciliation

CX03 inventories actual useful current project views and their real borrowers/owners. Quiesce,
migrate or regenerate pinned inputs/evidence, delete obsolete formats/runtime copies/readers and
compatibility-only helpers, and rerun only acceptance affected by cutover. Preserve actual current
retention/recovery consumers until owned drainage/disposal. No historical data, rollback store or
benchmark series is needed, and no shared/operator inspection/adoption is inferred.

Disposed i/j/k/l/r5 and prior schema/beta receipts retain the exact outcomes in coordinator §6;
original failed logical drain/Unknown does not become a pass. Absent selection files observed only
on owned j/k do not authorize inspecting other targets. New authoring creates no campaign or service.

CX04 then reconciles older efficiency/persisted/native/holistic/unified/SA9 owners against delivered
contracts and current evidence. Close only exact fulfilled obligations, explicitly retire historical-
only requirements lacking a current consumer, and preserve broader SA-F02/BC3/SM8/old interruption
work. Link maintenance during plan publication is not substantive completion or queue resumption.
Preserve the specific S04/F01↔N10/F05 overlap without importing N10's whole migration.

CO01 reports affected migrated producers/assets/consumers and scoped evidence to the coordinator.
It cannot close algorithm, semantic admission, cold or FastMCP acceptance by association. Required
combined delivery and decision dispositions precede CX04 handback; optional future platform
consumers retain explicit triggers rather than creating hidden remaining implementation scope.
