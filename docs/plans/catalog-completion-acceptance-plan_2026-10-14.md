## 1. What completion must establish

The target is a pinned API/evidence Catalog whose attributed facts, selected analyses and original
evidence survive independent admission and coherent unselected publication. Actual native consumers
must obtain those products under exact current authority. Explicit Behavioral Summary has its own
finite known answers; Catalog's authored NotRequested results remain distinct. A passing pure kernel,
successful producer body, completed native effect, clean host group, capture and enclosing fixture
are separate observations.

The [production companion](catalog-completion-production-plan_2026-10-14.md) supplies P01–P06's
corrected outcome/admission, resource, preparation and handoff contracts. The
[development companion](catalog-completion-development-plan_2026-10-14.md) supplies T01–T04's host
ownership, inspection and agent/evidence contracts. Coordinator §6 owns their current state. Acceptance consumes
working migrated slices, not their interface descriptions. Focused checks can proceed as their actual
prerequisites become available; optional inspection or agent conveniences do not block every product
test. A claim about composed host cleanup does require the corresponding delegated-owner contract.

The [friction review](../design_review/reviews/design_review_implementation-friction-target_2026-10-14.md)
and [preceding integrated source assessment](../design_review/reviews/design_review_catalog-creation-completion-integrated_2026-10-14.md)
identify distinct obligations. Preserve completed corrections while exercising the newly exposed
boundaries. In particular, actual nominal union validity must survive preparation factoring;
independently assured selectors and different physical copies must survive sharing; finality-only
reports must preserve real Outstanding/Unknown, storage and committed-effect facts.

No benchmark campaign is required. Static evidence can establish removed reconstruction or dead
allowance; runtime controls establish behavior under their actual conditions. Reservation totals are
not RSS, and durations from unrelated runs are not controlled speed comparisons.
