## 2. Combined target and reusable foundations

**Implemented baseline / enclosing acceptance open, 2026-10-14.** Exact dependency-ready scheduling,
compact Catalog owners, provider cancellation, Behavioral Flow streaming, keyed correspondence,
scratch/predecessor controls, lazy Summary correspondence and ADR-0169 analytic partial outcomes are
integrated at their recorded source/focused strengths. Private executed scoped validity, complete
search/lexical expectations, protected-source reuse, compound completion, bounded receiving,
canonical union/alias/transport preparation and ADR-0170 retirement are also source-integrated.
The final current-source native/Summary/Catalog/cold exits remain open. Reimplementing these
foundations from their old plans' original problem statements would discard completed work.

**Proposed correction.** Carry each property with its actual owner. Separate sticky operation failure
from joined ownership without flattening nested completion metadata. Optional capture must decline
before mandatory effects, or own and settle its effects independently. Admit nested growth before
mutation; charge retained backing once and scratch only while allocated. Borrow/share immutable
selectors and contributor ingredients under complete physical/resource context. Reuse compatible
direct assurance without changing model-owned dependency meaning or independent receiving admission.
Keep coherent handoff exclusion around mutable visibility, not unrelated optional private preparation.
Compose delegated host cleanup and capture writers before enclosing release; native finality remains
an independent authority. Match discovery detail to requested demand.

These are separate capabilities, not one universal lifecycle enum, workflow engine, proof cache,
allocator framework or persistent run database. Existing StateCharge/ResourceBudget, DataFusion
reservations, owned prepared runs, protected native capabilities and run/fixture/capture adapters
are suitable foundations for focused correction. Their existence does not establish the missing
composition. Library fit and exact consumer migrations are developed in the companions.

The workload includes full pinned releases/corpora/imports, reconvergent or cyclic relationships,
overlapping contributor prefixes, deep copy mappings and long retained run histories. Necessary flat
output/hash work and independent assurance are distinguished from repeated preparation. Benefits
follow the removed work/lifetime mechanisms and remain unmeasured. No benchmark campaign, arbitrary
job/thread cap, resource-limit increase or historical corpus is required. Genuinely comparable
evidence of a reasoned preferred design being more than 3× slower reopens its premise; different
bounds, host load or timeouts do not establish that ratio.
