## 2. Combined target and foundations

The workload includes a whole pinned library release, corpus and required import context; aliases,
inherited public APIs, ambiguous references, reconvergent/cyclic calls and evidence-dense artifacts
are legitimate. Typed transfer limits do not bound analyzer heaps. Scope windows do not eliminate
repeated lookup work within a window, and explicit refusal alone does not establish fit.

Retain model-owned interpretation and existing library composition: independent latest Ruff and
Pyrefly/ty observations; exact immutable completed selections; DataFusion bulk selection; charged
local indexes; petgraph projections/SCCs; BDD conditions; semantic delta/Pareto worklists; native
ordered runs, protected inventories and owned finality. Catalog's compact unrequested Behavioral
owners and demand-gated analytics remain meaningful distinctions.

Change the physical route in four related ways. Prepare correspondence once at its useful grain
and release scratch with its allocation. Expose actual semantic/invariant dependencies and assemble
contributions before consumers, without inheriting every unrelated publication-prefix barrier.
Carry genuinely executed same-operation properties across unchanged protected premises, while
checking final actual native state independently. Stream analyzer/module work and propagate
cancellation through the existing owners rather than accumulating results no consumer can use.

Semantic ownership does not require a physical stage/table/transaction per declared kind. Neither
does that observation justify removing actual freeze, assembly, scoped meaning or recovery fences.
No generic workflow engine, universal prepared-data object, second canonical store, process-global
proof cache, new service or arbitrary worker cap is selected. A simple charged map/queue or existing
library operator is preferred when sufficient. Nested pools around Pyrefly's shared pool require a
real benefit and feasible combined working set, not a presumption that more tasks means less time.

Expected benefits follow the removed work/dependency/lifetime mechanisms; they remain unmeasured.
Use first-principles route comparisons and revealing cases. Existing diagnostics or the required
pilot can settle a material premise; do not add a benchmark campaign. If genuinely comparable
evidence makes the reasoned preferred design more than3× slower, reopen its premise. A timeout,
parallel host load, nested spans or different bounds cannot establish that ratio.
