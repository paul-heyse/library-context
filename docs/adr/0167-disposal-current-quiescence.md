## Context

A wholly owned disposable Catalog campaign retains a dead ordinary-maintenance marker and an
unresolved attachment. Its recorded local cleanup does not independently prove current quiescence,
but missing historical marker-child ancestry need not make obsolete data permanently undisposable.
The operator approved current-quiescence transfer on 2026-10-13. ADR-0165's whole-target containment,
actual worker/executor drainage and independent outcome axes remain in force. This refines disposal,
not SOURCE_JOIN recovery, adoption or shared-service maintenance.

## Options

1. **Require reconstructed historical ancestry.** Conservative when that evidence exists, but adds
   an unavailable historical prerequisite even if every current supported participant is authenticated
   and quiescent. Keeping that requirement buys no additional protection against a live participant.
2. **Remove any dead marker and discard.** Simple but incorrect: process exit and cached cleanup do
   not establish current borrower, descendant, reader or executor quiescence.
3. **Transfer the narrow ordinary marker under fresh existing-owner checks.** Selected. Reuse
   service/run/attachment/storage ownership, journal the predecessor and independently establish
   current supported participant quiescence before exact daemon shutdown. This removes obsolete
   historical work without inventing a second lifecycle manager or weakening actual drainage.

## Decision

Only explicitly owned lifecycle-campaign disposal may supersede a strict private ordinary-maintenance
marker with a valid dead, non-foreign owner. Live, malformed, foreign and named recovery/restore/
reconciliation/handoff markers remain blocking. Under exclusive storage admission and coordination
locks, reload exact installation/generation/physical identities and authenticate the daemon, unit and
executable. Reobserve current managed run groups, attachment commands, development and enrolled
maintenance workers, and product-reader leases through their existing owners. Historical confirmed
cleanup and absent enrollment are not fresh proof. Older campaigns additionally require current
exact target/configuration/run bindings over supported launch paths; ambiguous or inaccessible
coverage blocks. No arbitrary host process is adopted as authority.

Record predecessor bytes/digest and fresh participant proof in the existing disposal journal before
atomic marker transfer. Retry reconciles that same predecessor or its matching disposal successor,
and rechecks participants. Keep admission closed, reauthenticate the executor immediately before
shutdown, and complete existing exact stop/discard and attachment/storage/credential release.
Failed logical drain and original native Unknown remain unchanged. Whole-target discard settles only
its disposal obligations after actual current local and executor quiescence.

Ordinary maintenance commands enroll target-bound managed ownership before spawn, including installer
children and yielded commands. Enrollment closes future observation coverage; it does not manufacture
historical ancestry or exempt existing campaigns from fresh current checks.
An explicitly supplied legacy managed-run reference adds an independently authenticated current
quiescence obligation. It never claims that the run targeted this campaign or descended from its
marker, and cannot replace mandatory attachment, host-selector or executor checks. Journal its
explicit provenance and preserve that obligation on retry.

## Consequences

Disposable state can be retired from current facts without retaining legacy data or reconstructing
historical execution. A stale marker alone never authorizes effects. Supported owner adapters and a
small journal extension carry the protection instead of new process infrastructure. Unknown current
quiescence remains blocking; unrelated authorized work can proceed on another owned target.

Implementation and focused acceptance belong to the [Catalog coordinator §6](../plans/catalog-completion-plan_2026-10-14.md#6-sole-disposition-and-current-checkpoint).
Focused host controls and actual campaign G checked disposal passed 2026-10-13 (`45058a`), including
ordinary-marker transfer, supplementary-run quiescence and every terminal cleanup step. Original
native Unknown remains unchanged. These controls do not establish unrelated recovery or product
composition. Revisit if a new supported participant cannot
be authenticated through existing enrollment/lease owners, or if shared-target transfer is proposed.
