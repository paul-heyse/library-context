## Context

The [upper execution coordinator](../plans/catalog-upper-execution-plan_2026-10-17.md#6-sole-disposition-and-current-checkpoint) owns UA01–UA04, US01/US02 and focused integration. The operator approved RC01/RC08 and this execution on 2026-10-17. UN/UC delivered protected preparation and exact windows. Remaining work repeats analytic searches and incidence preparation, retains dead Summary preparation, and orders independent work behind phase loops or immediate awaits.

## Options

1. Retain broad Facts/Normalized barriers and serial per-grain work. This limits transient state but delays complete independent consumers and repeats preparation.
2. Add a universal DAG engine, global cache, another pool or unchecked parallel kernels. These add operational machinery or lose complete authority, resource ownership and canonical integration.
3. Extend exact model requirements, immutable selected assurance and the existing registered task/native work owners. Selected. Small finite model jobs and ordinal private results express the actual remaining dependencies without a second scheduler or semantic authority.

## Decision

Replace only ADR-0174's whole Facts/Normalized execution-barrier clause with complete selected readiness. Its eager exact visibility, capture verification, per-relation private composition, optional-retention, independent-original and lexical-posting decisions remain in force under their existing owners. This is a scoped successor decision, not retirement of that multi-subject record. ADR-0179's joined producer guarantees extend to independent grains; ADR-0185/0186 retain protected native and exact consumer preparation.

One compiler worklist follows complete direct, invariant, assembly and authentic private-authority prerequisites. Selected semantic checking includes inferred ordinary facts formerly assured by the broad barrier. Exact consumer inputs, compact-owner qualification dependencies and contributor descriptors form a private immutable assurance snapshot; they grant no additional physical consumer ports. Selected check reuse includes every such dependency. Provider completion captures the provider-only facts availability views once. Later upper contributions cannot widen that checkpoint. Genuine coverage and shared-vocabulary dependencies remain, as do ordered whole-prefix closure and final complete admission/publication.

Independent typed work carries actual immutable input/preparation owners and the operation budget into existing registered tasks or native audit flights. Concrete task/result slots and known work/output reservations precede launch; deep results retain their own charges. Results integrate in existing semantic order. On failure, stop new work/integration and drain all accepted workers; dropped delivery never releases actual joins. Only a specifically refused launch allowance may progress through owned work that releases it; dynamic kernel refusal is terminal, never generic retry. Preserve existing input windows and normal available concurrency without another pool or worker cap.

Analytic correspondence indexes structural candidates without eagerly validating irrelevant rows. One compact attributed base feeds exact namespace scopes and independent FCA/RCA results; RCA additions remain separate. Finite owned technique jobs retain ranking, nearest-layer/community and label dependencies and fixed numerical reduction order. Keep correspondence's local mutable caches outside task transfer. Retain NextClosure/Duquenne–Guigues semantics and carry accepted extents rather than recomputing them.

Summary drops caller-attempt preparation at the final current-SCC borrower, retaining shared signatures, and releases solving-only state before terminal postprocessing. Whole frames may compute independently with immutable authentic Local/binding borrows. SCC solving, global member admission, progress and complete-frame publication remain ordered; no component streaming or resumable proof store is selected.

## Consequences

This removes source-established repeated work and unnecessary ordering while preserving exact domains, evidence, complete negative universes, independent receiving and actual cleanup. No dependency, public wire/schema, cutoff or deadline change is selected. Retained algorithm alternatives require a complete support/basis/partial-work/cancellation contract before reconsideration. Expected execution improvements are unmeasured.

Acceptance records direction, not implementation or verification. The coordinator owns actual findings and receipts. Full Catalog/assembled validation and historical timeout attribution remain separate. Protected historical Unknowns remain protected; no maintenance, regeneration, operator adoption or older-plan reconciliation follows from this decision.
