## Context

ADR-0121 selects attributed per-use native enumeration under the analytical-enrichment plan.
The genuine full-layer Structural fixture published both profiles, but its Behavioral named
handoff was absent: the consumer required CompleteUnderStatedModel for the entire artifact's
Flow family. EntryValueWitness has the same requirement. Conservative family coverage may be
Partial because another use loses precision, even when the requested use retains complete
native enumeration. Conversely, complete family coverage cannot certify a particular retained
candidate list. Source/interface inspection establishes this mismatch, 2026-10-04; corrected
runtime acceptance remains pending in the [coordinator §7](../plans/code-facts-analytical-enrichment-plan_2026-10-04.md#7-current-disposition-and-checkpoint).

This supersedes ADR-0111's global-family requirement for singleton origin admission. Its
checked-false retention policy and qualified finite-negative boundaries survive below.
ADR-0045 still owns original/runtime view correspondence, ADR-0120 the explicit premise basis,
and ADR-0121 the observation and bounded explanation contracts.

## Options

1. **Keep global complete coverage as the gate.** Simple, but unrelated losses suppress a
   locally complete origin and family status still does not replay the requested enumeration.
2. **Treat a visible singleton as complete.** Rejected: pruning or unmapped native candidates
   can fabricate uniqueness. A nonempty table is not a closure proof.
3. **Require the attributed complete per-use inventory and original exact premises** (chosen).
   The existing model replay owns candidate/mapped membership, digests and native/view attribution.
   Entry and handoff consumers share its bounded closure operation and retain its proof references.
   This adds declaration/hydration obligations without another provider or interpretation engine.

## Decision

Exact singleton Entry and named handoff origins require a complete, fully hydrated, publication-
replayed native inventory for the requested use, scope, source view, run and context, with its
native support and exact reaching member/support. Missing, incomplete, foreign, ambiguous,
pruned, loop-expanded or unmapped inventories refuse that proof. Keep the exact singleton,
unchanged formal or definition/place identity, non-loop-carried reaching, qualification,
condition implication and all existing original support requirements. No preferred candidate
or guarded multi-candidate proof is introduced. Family coverage is retained as attributed
evidence; CompleteUnderStatedModel is neither sufficient nor required for this per-use closure.

The checked-false lane retains a direct bare-name identity source only under its actual native
use and original exact-false expression condition. Computed values and call crossings retain
their refusal boundaries. Lower the entire nonempty, wholly exact-false native binding set;
retain every Defined/Undefined/Deleted state, including Unbound, and refuse loop headers.
Live or approximate sets keep the existing reaching-pruning policy; the new inventory exposes
that pruning as incomplete rather than silently granting closure. Missing, multiple, unbound
or unsupported formal origins remain unresolved. Retained candidates are not discarded skip
counts; native mapping source participates in provider identity.

A Refutation still concerns impossibility of an exact qualified finite alternative, never
whole-function transfer absence. Its exact-false condition, nominal source proof, invocation,
qualification and complete requested finite coverage/membership remain mandatory through
Summary/S0. This decision does not bypass their separate negative validators or strengthen
coverage. Partial family coverage and incomplete requested finite membership remain distinct.

## Consequences

Proof records cite inventory/support and existing premises through Synthesis and serving.
Shared publication replay and bounded hydration remain model-owned. The wire/schema migration
requires reviewed snapshots and matching native adapters. Earlier ADR-0111 receipts retain their
historical boundary; they do not certify the changed admission rule. Complete per-use inventory
under Partial family coverage must pass, while incomplete/foreign inventory must refuse, before
current qualification is closed. G2/G3 remain Deferred under the coordinator's native trigger.
No solver, alternate store, compatibility reader or whole-program completeness claim is added.
